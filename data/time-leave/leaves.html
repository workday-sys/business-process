Leave of Absence at a Glance
3 Core Business Processes · City Positions · Excluding Federal Work Study & State-Only Steps
🏖
Request Leave of Absence
Employee, Manager, HR Partner, Faculty Relations Partner, or Civil Service Director initiates a leave request that routes through review and approval.
🔙
Request Return from Leave
Employee or an HR/absence role initiates the return, entering the actual last day of absence, then routes for review.
✏️
Correct / Extend Leave
Standalone admin task (not a full approval flow) used to fix dates or push out an estimated end date on an existing leave.
Security Roles Legend
Who participates across all Leave of Absence processes
🔴 Employee 🟣 Manager 🟡 Absence Partner / Administrator 🔵 HR Partner / Administrator / Specialist ⚪ Benefits Partner 🌊 Payroll Partner (Central) 🟠 Timekeeper 🟢 Faculty Relations Partner / Civil Service Director
ℹ️
Multiple Roles Per PersonA person can wear multiple hats — an Absence Partner is very often the same person as the HR Partner at a campus. Roles are assigned per leave type, not exclusive.
⚠️
Two Separate Security Controls Per Leave TypeEach leave type controls (1) which employees are eligible to see/select it, and (2) which security groups can initiate it. Both are configured through leave type security segments — not every group listed can act on every leave type.
🔀
Leave of Absence vs. Time Off — Related but SeparateLeave of Absence covers longer, extended time away (medical, FMLA, fellowship, childcare, etc.). Short-term time off (vacation, sick days) is a separate process. Both live under the Absence umbrella in the system.
🚫
Excluded from this DashboardFederal Work Study positions and State-specific steps have been excluded. All flows shown apply to City positions only.

Request Leave of Absence

Initiated by Employee · Manager · HR Partner · Faculty Relations Partner · Civil Service Director

ApprovedAfter All Reviews
Who is Involved
  • Employee Requests own leave (if eligible to self-initiate)
  • Manager Can initiate on behalf; notified of outcome
  • Faculty Relations / Civil Service Can initiate for applicable populations
  • Absence Partner Reviews / approves
  • HR Partner Reviews / approves · assigns roles
  • Benefits Partner Changes benefit elections if flagged
  • Payroll Partner Final review when leave affects pay
Decision Flags
⚠️
Eligibility Drives the DropdownThe leave type list only shows types the employee is eligible for based on position — plus only the types that security allows the initiator to start.
🔀
Attachment May Be a Hard BlockIf a leave type requires documentation, submitting without an attachment throws a critical error. The system checks only that a file exists — reviewers validate the actual contents.

"On Leave" VisibilityOnly users secured to the leave event see the "on leave" flag next to the employee's name. The specific leave type stays private. Visibility rules are configurable (broader, or hidden entirely).

Paid Family Leave / Long-Term DisabilityThese leave types depend on a third-party carrier's eligibility decision before the benefit payment is confirmed. Exact placement of that step in the approval sequence is still being finalized.

Request Return from Leave of Absence

Initiated by Employee · Manager · Absence Administrator · Absence Partner · HR Administrator · HR Partner · HR Specialist · Timekeeper

ApprovedAfter All Reviews
Who is Involved
  • Employee Can initiate own return
  • Manager Can initiate on behalf
  • Absence Partner / Administrator Can initiate; reviews
  • HR Partner / Administrator / Specialist Can initiate; assigns roles
  • Timekeeper Can initiate on behalf
  • Benefits Partner Changes benefits if flagged
  • Payroll Partner Reviews; reinstates pay
Decision Flags
⚠️
No Automatic ReturnIf no return is entered, the employee stays "on leave" indefinitely past the estimated last day — the system will not auto-return them unless specifically configured to.
🔀
Attachment Rules Are IndependentThe return action has its own, separate attachment rule from the original request — e.g. a doctor's note or FMLA return-to-work form can be required here even if it wasn't required going out on leave.

📅
Extending a LeaveAn extension is not a brand-new request — it's handled either as a Correction to the estimated last day (admin task, see Correct / Extend tab) or by entering a later actual/estimated date directly on the return. An audit trail tracks the change either way.

Correct / Extend Leave

Standalone admin task — not part of an approval business process

Triggered byIncorrect Entry or Extension
Who is Involved
  • Absence Partner / Administrator Can correct
  • HR Partner / Administrator Can correct
  • Timekeeper Can correct (where scoped)
  • Manager No access — admin task only
Decision Flags
🚫
Not an Approval FlowCorrection is a direct edit, not a reviewed business process — there is no second set of eyes. Access should be tightly scoped through security since it bypasses approval.
🔀
Full Audit Trail RetainedEven though there's no approval step, every correction is tracked — the original and updated dates remain visible for later review.

Role Definitions
Security role descriptions for Leave of Absence scope
Role Description Can Wear Other Hats?
Employee The worker requesting their own leave or return, where self-service initiation is allowed for that leave type. Cannot approve their own request. N/A
Manager Can initiate a leave or return on behalf of a direct report. Reviews/acknowledges leave requests — full approval authority for medical/FMLA leaves is being reconsidered in favor of a notification or acknowledgment step. ✔ Can also be Timekeeper, HR Partner, Absence Partner
Absence Partner / Administrator Campus HR-based role that reviews and approves leave and return requests. Frequently the same individual as the HR Partner — when they match, the duplicate approval step is skipped automatically. ✔ Very often also HR Partner, Timekeeper, or Benefits Partner
HR Partner / Administrator / Specialist Reviews and approves requests, assigns security roles triggered by a leave, and can initiate on an employee's behalf. Based at the campus. ✔ Can also be Absence Partner, Manager, Timekeeper
Benefits Partner Changes benefit elections when a leave request is flagged with a benefit impact. Only involved when that specific condition is triggered. ✔ Can also be HR Partner or Absence Partner
Payroll Partner Central payroll role — not typically at the campus level. Final reviewer whenever a leave changes pay (unpaid, reduced %, fellowship adjustments). Processes the action/reason that feeds City and State payroll integrations. Not involved when a leave keeps the employee at full pay, unless pay later changes. Central role — generally not combined with campus roles
Timekeeper Can initiate a return from leave on behalf of an employee. Currently not enabled to initiate a Request Leave of Absence in the test tenant — flagged as a configuration item pending campus input on which leave types should be in scope. ✔ Can also be Manager, HR Partner, Absence Partner
Faculty Relations Partner / Civil Service Director Can initiate a leave request for applicable faculty/civil service populations (e.g. scholarly or fellowship leave). Committee and presidential approvals for scholarly leave happen outside the system; this role's involvement is limited to initiation and coordination. Role is population-specific, not typically combined
🚫
Excluded: Federal Work Study & State PositionsFederal Work Study coordination and State-specific roles/steps are not included in this dashboard. These operate under a separate scope and process.
