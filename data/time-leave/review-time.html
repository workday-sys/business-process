Review Time & Mass Operations at a Glance
3 Core Business Processes · City Positions · Excluding Federal Work Study & State-Only Steps
🔍
Review & Approve Time
Management Chain, Manager, or Timekeeper reviews submitted time in the Time & Scheduling Hub and approves it.
📝
Mass Enter Time
Timekeeper or Time Administrator enters time for a group of employees at once — up to 100 at a time.
📤
Mass Submit Time
Timekeeper or Time Administrator submits already-entered time in bulk for an employee group or organization.
🔔
Period Mgmt & Alerts
Central Office controls when time periods open, lock, unlock, and close; automated alerts flag missing steps.
Security Roles Legend
Who participates across Review Time and Mass Operations
🔴 Employee 🟣 Manager / Management Chain 🟠 Timekeeper 🟢 Time Administrator 🔵 HR Partner 🌊 Payroll Administrator (Central Office)
ℹ️
Multiple Roles Per PersonA person can wear multiple hats — a Timekeeper can also be a Manager, HR Partner, or hold central Time Administrator access. Roles are assigned, not exclusive.
⚠️
Access Scope Drives What You Can DoManagers and Timekeepers can only review, enter, or submit time for workers within their assigned organization. A manager sitting at the top of an org (e.g., a manager's manager) can drill down into subordinate organizations. Mass Submit / Mass Approve capability also exists as a related action, generally limited to Timekeepers.
🔀
Mass Operations vs. EIBs — Two Different ToolsMass Operations (Mass Enter Time / Mass Submit Time) are UI-driven tasks run directly in Workday by Timekeepers and Time Administrators. Enterprise Interface Builders (EIBs) are separate Excel-based bulk data loads, generally handled by the central team — not covered in this dashboard.
🚫
Excluded from this DashboardFederal Work Study positions and State-specific steps have been excluded. All flows shown apply to City positions only.

Mass Submit Time

Performed by Timekeeper · Time Administrator

Approval Due2 Business Days
Who is Involved
  • Timekeeper Runs mass submission for entered time in their org
  • Time Administrator Default recipient for approval of mass-submitted time
Decision Flags
⚠️
Critical Errors Block SubmissionAny entry with a critical error or unresolved warning is excluded from the mass submission and needs attention first.
🔀
Routing Is ConfigurableBy default, time submitted in mass routes to the Time Administrator for approval. This routing can be reconfigured so it instead goes to the Timekeeper.

Time Period Management & Alerts
Central Office Oversight · Applies Across Review Time and Mass Operations
📅
PurposeEach pay period has an open date, a lock date, an unlock (adjustment) window, and a close date that control when time entry and submission are allowed.
Who Manages Periods
  • Payroll Administrator Central Office sets & maintains period dates
  • Time Administrator May be exempt from lockout for corrections
Automated Alerts (Run Weekly)
  • 📋 No time entered
  • ⏳ Time entered but not submitted
  • ⚠️ Time submitted but not approved
⚠️
Period Locking Affects PayrollLocking, unlocking, and closing a period affects not just time entry but also accruals and the payroll run itself — this is why it is centrally controlled rather than managed at the campus level.
🔀
Exempt GroupsA specific role/group can be designated as exempt from lockout, allowing adjustments to be made even after a time period has locked.

Role Definitions
Security role descriptions for Review Time & Mass Operations scope
Role Description Can Wear Other Hats?
Employee The worker whose time is being reviewed, entered, or submitted. Receives automated alerts if time has not been entered or submitted. N/A
Manager / Management Chain Reviews and approves time for direct reports. A manager positioned above other organizations can drill down into subordinate organizations to review and approve there as well. ✔ Can also be Timekeeper or HR Partner
Timekeeper Administrative role that can review, approve, mass enter, and mass submit time on behalf of employees within their assigned organization. Typically the primary user of Mass Operations at the campus level. ✔ Can also be Manager, HR Partner, or hold Time Administrator access
Time Administrator Broader administrative access, typically at a more central level. Default recipient for approval of time submitted through Mass Submit Time. Can run Mass Operations across a wider organizational scope than a campus Timekeeper. ✔ Can hold other roles
HR Partner Can be involved in schedule setup and modification (e.g., adjusting a worker's assigned work schedule). Visibility into time records is governed by domain security policy. ✔ Can also be Timekeeper
Payroll Administrator (Central Office) Sets and maintains the open, lock, unlock, and close dates for each pay period. Responsible because period status affects accruals and payroll processing, not just time entry. ✔ Central Office role
🚫
Excluded: Federal Work Study & State PositionsFWS time tracking and State-specific roles are not included in this dashboard. These operate under a separate scope and process.
