Personal Information Changes III — At a Glance
4 Core Business Processes · Excluding Federal Work Study & State-Only Steps
📝
Legal Name Change
Employee or an HR-side initiator submits a legal name change with supporting documentation; routes to HR Partner for review when initiated by someone else.
🖼️
Photo Change
Employee or HR uploads a worker profile photo. Optional, single-step, auto-completes — no review or approval configured.
✏️
Preferred Name Change
Employee or an HR-side initiator updates a preferred first/middle name. Last name cannot be changed here — blocked by validation.
🌐
Public Profile Preferences
Employee only — opts individual fields (date of birth, pronoun, sexual orientation, education) in or out of visibility to co-workers on the same campus.

What "Employee (Self)" CoversFor simplicity, this dashboard groups Employee, Pre-Employee, Contingent Worker, and Pre-Contingent Worker — each acting "as self" — into one Employee (Self) role, since they behave identically in every process below.

Legal Name Change

Initiated by Employee (Self) · HR Administrator · HR Partner · Recruiter · Recruiting Administrator

Completes WhenHR Partner Reviews & Approves*
Who is Involved
  • Employee (Self) Initiates own legal name change; must attach documentation
  • HR Administrator Can initiate on behalf of another worker
  • HR Partner Can initiate on behalf; reviews & approves when initiated by another (non-student job family)
  • Recruiter Can initiate on behalf of another worker
  • Recruiting Administrator Can initiate on behalf of another worker
Decision Flags
⚠️
Critical Validation — Documentation RequiredIf any part of the legal first or last name is changing, a supporting document (e.g. Social Security card) must be attached or the business process throws a hard error and will not submit. Changing only a prefix (Mr./Ms./Dr.) does not trigger this validation.
🔀
Self-Initiated vs. Initiated by AnotherWhen the employee initiates on themselves, the process completes on submission. When HR Administrator, HR Partner, Recruiter, or Recruiting Administrator initiates on behalf of someone else, it routes to an HR Partner for review and approval.
🚫
Known Routing Defect (Logged in Testing)When an HR Partner submits a legal name change on their own record, the review task has been observed routing back to that same HR Partner instead of a different HR Partner or manager — unlike other personal information changes, where it correctly skips to someone else. Flagged as a defect; routing rules are being checked against the standard Personal Information Change routing logic.

Photo Change

Initiated by Employee (Self) · HR Administrator · HR Partner

Completes WhenInitiator Submits
Who is Involved
  • Employee (Self) Uploads own worker profile photo — optional
  • HR Administrator Can upload, override, or remove a worker's photo
  • HR Partner Can upload, override, or remove a worker's photo
Decision Flags
⚠️
Single Step — Auto-CompletesThere is only an initiation step. As soon as the initiator hits submit, the business process is complete and the photo shows on the worker profile — no review or approval step exists today.
🔀
Optional EverywhereUploading a photo is never required. It is also part of onboarding, where the task can be skipped entirely.
🚫
No Content Validation, No Approval RoutingWorkday validates only file size, not photo content — it cannot detect whether an image is an appropriate headshot. Because there is no approval step, HR is not notified who uploaded a given photo. Raised in walkthrough as a gap worth an approval step to prevent inappropriate or non-professional images; logged as feedback for design consideration.

Initiated by Employee (Self) · HR Administrator · HR Partner · Recruiter · Recruiting Administrator

  • HR Partner Can initiate on behalf of another worker
  • Recruiter Can initiate on behalf of another worker
  • Recruiting Administrator Can initiate on behalf of another worker
Decision Flags
⚠️
Critical Validation — Last Name Cannot Change HereProposed last name must match the current last name, or the business process throws a hard error. This process only allows first (and middle) name changes; any last name change must go through Legal Name Change instead.
🔀
Single Step — No Review Configured TodayOnly an initiation step exists; the process auto-completes on submission regardless of who initiates it. In the walkthrough session, attendees raised adding an HR Partner review/approval step (similar to Legal Name Change) as a possible enhancement — logged as an open item, not yet built.

Public Profile Preferences

Initiated by Employee (Self) only — no on-behalf-of initiation

Completes WhenEmployee Submits
Who is Involved
  • Employee (Self) Only role that can initiate — no HR or admin on-behalf-of option
Decision Flags
⚠️
Default Is HiddenEvery field (date of birth, pronoun, sexual orientation, education) is hidden from other workers by default. Nothing is visible until the employee actively opts a field in.
🔀
Campus-Scoped, Not Public Internet"Public" here means visible to other workers within the same campus/company in Workday — not the public internet or a college website. Security is segregated by campus, so a worker at one campus cannot search up a worker at another.
ℹ️
Reversible AnytimeAn employee who opts a field in can return to the task and hide it again at any time — nothing is permanent.

Role Definitions
Security role descriptions for Personal Information Changes scope
Role Description Can Wear Other Hats?
Employee (Self) Covers Employee, Pre-Employee, Contingent Worker, and Pre-Contingent Worker acting "as self." Initiates changes to their own record. Cannot approve their own Legal Name Change when it requires review. N/A
HR Administrator Can initiate Legal Name Change, Photo Change, and Preferred Name Change on behalf of another worker. Does not participate in Public Profile Preferences. ✔ Can also hold HR Partner responsibilities depending on campus setup
HR Partner Can initiate on behalf of another worker across most processes, and is the reviewer/approver for Legal Name Changes initiated by someone other than the employee. Can also override or remove a worker's photo. ✔ Can also hold HR Administrator responsibilities
Recruiter Can initiate Legal Name Change and Preferred Name Change on behalf of another worker — typically used for candidates transitioning into worker records. ✔ Can also hold Recruiting Administrator responsibilities
Recruiting Administrator Broader recruiting-side administrative access. Can initiate Legal Name Change and Preferred Name Change on behalf of another worker. ✔ Can also hold Recruiter responsibilities
🚫
Excluded: Federal Work Study Coordinator & State PositionsFederal Work Study coordinator review/routing and State-specific roles are not included in this dashboard. These operate under a separate scope and process.
