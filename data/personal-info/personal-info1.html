Personal Information Changes I — At a Glance
4 Core Business Processes · Faculty/Staff Scope · Excluding Federal Work Study & State-Only Steps
🪪
Edit Government IDs
Employee, Pre-Employee, HR Partner, or HR Administrator submits/updates a Social Security Number or ITIN.
🗂️
Edit ID Information
HR Partner makes a one-step, all-in-one update across Government IDs, Passports, Visas, Licenses, and Other IDs.
🧾
Edit Other IDs
HR Partner or Payroll Administrator updates custom payroll-integration IDs (e.g. NYC Employee ID).
🛂
Passports & Visa Change
Employee, Pre-Employee, Contingent Worker, or HR staff submits a passport/visa update with supporting document.
Security Roles Legend
Who participates across all Personal Information Changes I processes
🔴 Employee / Pre-Employee (Self) 🟠 Contingent Worker / Pre-Contingent Worker (Self) 🔵 HR Partner ⚫ HR Administrator 🌊 Payroll Administrator
ℹ️
One Task, Many ID TypesEdit ID Information gives HR Partners (and, where security allows, HR Administrators) an all-in-one page to update Government IDs, Passports, Visas, Licenses, and Other IDs in a single submission — instead of running each business process separately.
⚠️
Auto-Approval When Initiator = ApproverIf the person submitting the change is themselves an HR Partner for that organization, the approval step is skipped and the process auto-completes. This is the "No Current Assignee was Initiator or Prior Approver" condition rule seen on every approval step.
🔀
Job Family Routing — Student vs. Non-StudentEvery approval step checks whether the worker's Job Family is Student Employee. Non-student (Faculty/Staff) changes route to the HR Partner, shown in this dashboard. Student Employee changes route to the Federal Work Study Coordinator, which is out of scope here.
🔒
Access Is Security-DrivenEach task is only visible to the security roles assigned to it. If a step doesn't appear for someone who expects it, that usually means their security role mapping needs to be requested/updated rather than a system error.
🚫
Excluded: Federal Work Study & State PositionsFederal Work Study routing (Federal Work Study Coordinator) and State-specific steps are not included in this dashboard. These operate under a separate scope and process.

Critical SSN Validation RulesMultiple validations block impossible Social Security Number combinations (e.g. certain digit patterns are rejected) before the record can be submitted.

Not Used by Contingent WorkersThis task is not part of the security scope for Contingent Workers — that population is handled under a separate contingent worker/POI process covered elsewhere.

Passports & Visa Change

Initiated by Employee (Self) · Pre-Employee (Self) · Contingent Worker (Self) · Pre-Contingent Worker (Self) · HR Partner · HR Administrator

Completes WhenHR Partner Approves
Who is Involved
  • Employee (Self) Initiator only
  • Pre-Employee (Self) Initiator only
  • Contingent Worker (Self) Initiator only — can start, cannot approve/submit further
  • Pre-Contingent Worker (Self) Initiator only
  • HR Partner Initiator & approver
  • HR Administrator Initiator only
Decision Flags
⚠️
Document Required Before SubmittingA supporting document must be attached to the passport/visa change, or the critical validation will block submission.
⚠️
Cannot Hold a Passport and F-1 Visa at the Same TimeThe validation is date-driven — a worker cannot have an active (US) passport and an active F-1 visa recorded simultaneously. Currently only the US passport type is covered by this rule.
🔀
Contingent Workers Are Initiators OnlyThis is the one process in this scope where Contingent Workers and Pre-Contingent Workers participate — as self-service initiators only.

Role Definitions
Security role descriptions for Personal Information Changes I scope
Role Description Approval Authority
Employee (Self) The worker updating their own information through self-service. Covers Government IDs and Passports & Visa Change only. – Initiator only
Pre-Employee (Self) A not-yet-started worker completing the same self-service ID tasks ahead of their start date. – Initiator only
Contingent / Pre-Contingent Worker (Self) Non-employee workers (and pre-hire contingent workers). Only participate in Passports & Visa Change, as self-service initiators — not part of Government IDs, ID Information, or Other IDs in this scope. – Initiator only
HR Partner Primary approver across all four processes. Can also initiate on behalf of a worker; when the HR Partner is both initiator and would-be approver, the process auto-completes. ✔ Primary approver
HR Administrator Administrative initiator on Government IDs and Passports & Visa Change; does not approve. Access is user-based, so notification/visibility can be scoped down by campus/org. – Initiator only
Payroll Administrator Initiates changes to custom/payroll-integration IDs under Edit Other IDs. Approval routes to the HR Partner. – Initiator only
⚠️
Common Condition Rule"No Current Assignee was Initiator or Prior Approver" appears on every approval step in this scope — it prevents an HR Partner from having to approve a change they just submitted themselves.
🗂️
Data Quality NoteBecause some worker types (e.g. certain contingent/volunteer records) may not initially capture an SSN or TIN, duplicate worker records are a known risk when that person later becomes a CUNY employee. Search carefully before creating a new record.
