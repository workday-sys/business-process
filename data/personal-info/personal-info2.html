Interactive guide to Home Contact, Work Contact, Personal Information & Veteran Status workflows — roles, steps, decisions, and approvals · City positions only

Personal Information Changes II — At a Glance
4 Core Business Processes · City (Faculty/Staff) Positions · Excluding Federal Work Study & State-Only Steps
🏠
Home Contact Change
Worker or HR staff update home address, phone, e-mail, or messenger info. Requires supporting documentation.
💼
Work Contact Change
HR Administrator or HR Partner updates work phone, work e-mail, and work address on the worker profile.
🪪
Personal Information Change
Update date of birth, gender, marital status, citizenship, race/ethnicity, or pronoun. Sensitive fields require documentation.
🎖
Veteran Status Identification
Worker or HR staff records/updates the employee's veteran status self-identification. Single step, no approval.
Security Roles Legend
Who participates across all Personal & Employment Data Change processes
🔴 Self / Worker (Employee, Pre-Employee, Terminee, Retiree, Contingent Worker, Pre-Contingent Worker) 🟠 HR Administrator 🔵 HR Partner
ℹ️
Multiple Roles Per PersonA person can be an HR Administrator and an HR Partner, or hold a self-service worker role at the same time as an administrative role. Roles are assigned, not exclusive.
⚠️
Self-Approval Skip LogicEvery approval step across these four processes shares the same condition: it only routes to an HR Partner "If Job Family is not Student Employee and No Current Assignee was Initiator or Prior Approver." In plain terms — if the HR Partner who is submitting the change would also be the one approving it, the step auto-completes instead of routing back to them.
🔀
Documentation Is the Big Swing FactorHome Contact Change and Personal Information Change both carry a "critical validation" requiring an uploaded document before submission — but only for specific fields (see each process tab). Work Contact Change and Veteran Status Identification require no document at all.
🚫
Excluded from this DashboardFederal Work Study positions and State-specific steps/roles have been excluded. All flows shown apply to City (Faculty/Staff) positions only.
Clarifications Captured From the Walkthrough Session
Questions & answers from the review session, used to clarify how the design is intended to work
🗣️
Address Documentation Is New — Flagged by Multiple TestersSeveral reviewers (Michelle Deleon, Keisha Pottinger-Moore) pushed back that no documentation is required today for an address change and asked why it's now a critical validation. The presenter confirmed it was added based on prior review-cycle feedback and asked testers to log a defect if they feel it should be removed.
🗣️
Personal Information Documentation Also QuestionedMichelle Deleon separately noted that requiring HR Partners to attach a document feels redundant, since Workday is not the system of record for those documents in the first place. Logged as a defect candidate rather than a confirmed change.
🗣️
County FieldCounty is a required field for pre-hire records (needed for payroll integration). For an existing employee's address change it is not currently required, and whether it should be is still an open discussion with the Payroll team.
🗣️
Multi-Position EmployeesPersonal information (address, DOB, marital status, etc.) is tied to the worker profile, not to an individual position — so it stays consistent across all of a worker's positions. Routing of the approval, however, is position-driven (e.g., which organization/HR Partner owns that position).
🗣️
Notifications Not Yet ConfiguredThe presenter confirmed notifications have not been built out in this tenant yet, so "who gets notified" could not be demonstrated live and requirements still need to be gathered.

Home Contact Change

Initiated by Self (Employee · Pre-Employee · Terminee · Retiree · Contingent Worker · Pre-Contingent Worker) · HR Administrator · HR Partner

Completes AtHR Partner Approval
Who is Involved
  • Self Employee, Pre-Employee, Terminee, or Retiree can initiate their own change
  • Self Contingent Worker or Pre-Contingent Worker can initiate their own change
  • HR Administrator Can initiate on behalf of a worker
  • HR Partner Can initiate and is the sole approver
Decision Flags
⚠️
Critical Validation — Documentation RequiredAny initiator — worker, HR Administrator, or HR Partner — must upload a supporting document for an address change. Added from prior review-cycle feedback; multiple testers questioned whether this should remain a requirement.
🔀
Self-Approval SkipApproval (Step BA) only routes to an HR Partner if the job family is not Student Employee and the current assignee was not already the initiator or a prior approver. If the submitting HR Partner would be the approver, it auto-completes.

VisibilityContact info marked "Private" is hidden from other workers, but HR staff can still see it regardless of the visibility setting. Multiple usage tags (e.g., Shipping, Permanent, Billing) can be applied to one address instead of entering it multiple times.

Documentation Required — Four Fields OnlyA document upload is required only when Date of Birth, Gender, Marital Status, or Citizenship Status is being changed. Race, ethnicity, sexual orientation, and pronoun are optional fields with no document requirement.

To Do — Update Tax ElectionsRoutes to the employee only if their marital status changed and the change is not coming from the Onboarding process. It's a manual notification/task — the employee updates tax elections themselves; the business process completes at approval regardless of whether the To Do is done.

Role Definitions
Security role descriptions for Personal & Employment Data Changes — City position scope
Role Description Can Wear Other Hats?
Self / Worker Covers Employee, Pre-Employee, Terminee, Retiree, Contingent Worker, and Pre-Contingent Worker acting as themselves. Can initiate changes to their own home contact, personal information, and veteran status. Cannot approve their own submission. N/A
HR Administrator Administrative role that can initiate Home Contact, Work Contact, Personal Information, and Veteran Status changes on behalf of a worker. Does not hold an approval step in any of these four processes. ✔ Can also be HR Partner
HR Partner Can initiate any of the four processes and is the sole approver on Home Contact Change, Work Contact Change, and Personal Information Change. Skips their own approval step when they are also the initiator (self-approval skip logic). ✔ Can also be HR Administrator
🚫
Excluded: Federal Work Study & State PositionsFederal Work Study Coordinator routing and State-specific roles/steps are not included in this dashboard. These operate under a separate scope and process.
