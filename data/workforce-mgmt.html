Assigned vs. Default by Eligibility RuleWork schedules can either be manually assigned to a worker (this dashboard's flow) or automatically defaulted based on a centrally-configured eligibility rule — the same principle used for holiday calendars.

Assign Work Schedule

Initiated by Employee/Contingent Worker (Self) · Manager · Timekeeper · HR Administrator/Partner · Absence Administrator/Partner · Management Chain · Top-Level Manager's Approver

Overall / Approval2 Business Days Each
Who is Involved
  • Manager Most common initiator; approval satisfied automatically when Manager initiates
  • Timekeeper Can initiate on behalf of an employee
  • HR Administrator / Partner Can initiate on behalf of an employee
  • Absence Administrator / Partner Can initiate on behalf of an employee
  • Management Chain / Top-Level Approver Can initiate on behalf of an employee
  • Employee / Contingent Worker Can initiate for self where enabled
  • Time Tracking Administrator Not an initiator on this BP — maintains the central library of work schedule calendars used by everyone above
Decision Flags
⚠️
Manager-Initiated = Auto-ApprovedBecause the BP's approval step is "Approval by Manager," when the Manager is the one who initiates the assignment, that step is already satisfied — no separate, redundant approval task is generated.
🔀
Calendars Are Selected, Not BuiltInitiators pick from an existing library of work schedule calendars (e.g., a pattern of days/hours/meal times). New calendars are created and maintained centrally to prevent duplicates across campuses.

Correction & Rescind AvailableIf the wrong schedule or start date is selected, the initiating roles (per configured security) can Correct the existing assignment or Rescind it entirely — there is no need to start over from scratch.
🔀
Assignment Is Per Worker, Not Per PositionA work schedule calendar is assigned at the worker level, not the position level. Employees holding multiple positions still receive a single schedule assignment; position-specific hours are tracked separately on the position record.
🚫
Unscheduled-Day Entries Are Not BlockedAs currently designed, the system does not hard-stop an employee from entering or approving time on a day outside their assigned pattern — a configurable warning message can be added if needed during testing.

Time Off Balance Transfer

Initiated by Absence Administrator/Partner · HR Administrator/Partner · Management Chain · Manager · Top-Level Manager's Approver

Overall / Approval2 Business Days Each
Who is Involved
  • Absence Partner Initiates and is the designated approver on the governed BP
  • Absence Administrator Can initiate
  • HR Administrator / Partner Can initiate
  • Manager Can initiate
  • Management Chain / Top-Level Approver Can initiate
Decision Flags
⚠️
Override Balance vs. Time Off AdjustmentOverriding a balance simply resets the visible amount on a plan — it does not notify Payroll. If hours must be communicated to Payroll (e.g., a payout), that is a separate Time Off Adjustment task, not covered in this walkthrough.
🔀
Effective Date Is Locked to the 1st of the MonthThe override date is driven by the plan's monthly period schedule, so only the first of an eligible month can be selected.

Role Definitions
Security role descriptions for this scope
Role Description Can Wear Other Hats?
Employee / Contingent Worker The worker whose schedule or balance is being managed. May initiate their own schedule assignment where self-service is enabled. Not an initiator or approver on Time Off Balance Transfer. N/A
Manager Primary approver on Assign Work Schedule; approval is automatically satisfied when the Manager is also the initiator. Can also initiate a Time Off Balance Transfer. ✔ Can also be Timekeeper, HR Partner, Absence Partner
Timekeeper Administrative role that can initiate a schedule assignment on behalf of an employee. Not listed as a participant on Time Off Balance Transfer. ✔ Can also be Manager, HR Partner, Absence Partner
HR Administrator / HR Partner Can initiate either process on behalf of an employee. Involved in visibility and coordination across campuses. ✔ Can also be Timekeeper or Manager
Absence Administrator / Absence Partner Owns the absence/time-off side of both processes. The Absence Partner is the designated approver on the governed Time Off Balance Transfer BP. Absence Administrators also help maintain centrally-built work schedule calendars. ✔ Can also be HR Partner or Timekeeper
Time Tracking Administrator Broad administrative access to the schedule calendar library. Builds and maintains the shared work schedule calendars that Managers, Timekeepers, and others select from — not an initiator of an individual assignment. ✔ Can hold other roles
Management Chain / Top-Level Manager's Approver Escalation-level roles that can initiate either process, typically used when the direct Manager is unavailable or when a higher-level review applies. ✔ Can also hold other roles
Clarifications & Open Items
Captured from Q&A during the configuration review session · March 12th, 2026
⚠️
Governed BP vs. Task Behavior (Time Off Balance Transfer)The documented BP shows an approval step by the Absence Partner. During the live walkthrough, the "Maintain Time Off Plan Override Balances" screen updated the balance immediately on clicking OK, with no approval routing shown. Recommend confirming during testing which behavior applies for your intended use cases.
🔀
Off-Schedule Time EntryIf an employee works hours outside their assigned pattern for a given day, the system currently allows the entry and generates a warning (not a hard block) for both the employee and approver. This can be reconfigured to a hard block if needed.
🔀
Calendar Naming ConventionTo help distinguish part-time vs. full-time calendars in the selection list (and to support future validation rules), a naming convention such as a "PT-" / "FT-" prefix was discussed as a recommendation to the central scheduling team.
📋
Who Can Assign Schedules — Security Group FeedbackSome campuses noted that HR and Timekeeper roles currently assign work schedules locally (not only Managers). This was flagged to the project team as a potential security group update to review.
📦
High-Volume Assignment (EIB)For bulk schedule changes affecting many employees at once (e.g., a seasonal schedule shift), an Enterprise Interface Builder (EIB) template load is available for admin-level roles, with the option to auto-approve or route through the normal approval step.
🧮
Cumulative Hour Caps for Certain Hourly TitlesA question was raised about warning an employee/approver as they approach a contractual cumulative hours cap over a fiscal year or semester (rather than a single week). This is likely better addressed through reporting/alerting than a hard validation, and remains an open discussion item.
🚫
Scope NoteThis dashboard reflects City-title, standard population workflows only. Population-specific approval variations outside this scope are not detailed here.
