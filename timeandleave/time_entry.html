Time & Leave — Time Entry at a Glance
4 Core Business Processes · City Positions · Excluding Federal Work Study & State-Only Steps
⏱
Enter Time
Employee, Manager, Timekeeper, or Time Tracking Administrator submits a timesheet for the week.
🔄
Approve Time
Manager or Timekeeper reviews submitted timesheet and approves, sends back, or closes.
✏️
Edit / Correct Time
Manager or Timekeeper adjusts clock-in/out block events on behalf of non-exempt employees.
🕐
Request Absence
Employee requests time off (vacation, sick, etc.) through the absence calendar. Goes to Manager for approval.
Security Roles Legend
Who participates across all Time Entry processes
🔴 Employee 🟣 Manager 🟠 Timekeeper 🟢 Time Tracking Administrator 🔵 HR Partner 🌊 Payroll Partner
ℹ️
Multiple Roles Per PersonA person can wear multiple hats — a Timekeeper can also be a Manager, Payroll Partner, Benefits Officer, or HR person. Roles are assigned, not exclusive.
⚠️
Who Enters Time? Depends on Employee TypeNon-exempt (hourly) employees clock in/out via a web clock — they cannot self-edit times. HEOs/HEAs self-report start/end times (microblock edit) for comp time/OT purposes. Managers and Timekeepers can enter or correct time on behalf of employees.
🔀
Time Entry vs. Time Off — Two Separate ProcessesTime Entry covers worked hours (timesheets). Time Off (absence) is a separate module — vacation, sick, disability, etc. go through Request Absence on the absence calendar. Approved absences automatically appear on the timesheet.
🚫
Excluded from this DashboardFederal Work Study positions and State-specific steps have been excluded. All flows shown apply to City positions only.

Workday Reporting · All City Positions · Used by Managers, Timekeepers, and Admins

PurposeWorkday provides reports to track pending tasks, overdue timesheets, and time entry status. These help Managers and Timekeepers ensure all time is entered and approved on schedule.
Who Can View
  • Manager Sees team time status
  • Timekeeper Monitors group timesheets
  • Time Tracking Admin Broad org-level reporting
  • HR Partner Audit visibility
  • Payroll Partner Final payroll review
What Can Be Tracked
  • 📋 Pending timesheet submissions
  • ⏳ How long a task has been sitting
  • ⚠️ Hours not matching scheduled hours
  • 📅 Overdue approvals by due date
  • 🔍 Approved vs. unapproved by week
⚠️
Proactive Monitoring RecommendedIf time is submitted and approval is not completed within the 2-day due period, Managers/Timekeepers should use reports to identify and action pending tasks — not wait for escalation.

Role Definitions
Security role descriptions for Time & Leave — Time Entry scope
Role Description Can Wear Other Hats?
Employee The worker submitting their own time. Non-exempt uses web clock; HEO/HEA uses microblock self-report. Cannot approve their own time. N/A
Manager Approves time for their direct reports. Can also initiate and enter time on behalf of employees. Receives approval tasks in Workday inbox. ✔ Can also be Timekeeper, HR Partner, Payroll Partner
Timekeeper Administrative role that can enter, correct, and approve time on behalf of employees. Often used when a department has a dedicated admin managing timesheets. ✔ Can also be Manager, Payroll Partner, HR Partner, Benefits Officer
Time Tracking Administrator Broader administrative access. Can initiate time entry on behalf of anyone in their scope. Typically manages setup, org-level reporting, and troubleshooting. ✔ Can hold other roles
HR Partner Involved in audit and visibility of time records. Not typically an initiator or approver in time entry, but has read access for compliance and reporting. ✔ Can also be Timekeeper or Manager
Payroll Partner Receives final processed time and amounts after approval. Manages the Payserv/PMS data flow. Not an initiator or approver. ✔ Can also be Timekeeper
🚫
Excluded: Federal Work Study & State PositionsFWS time tracking and State-specific roles are not included in this dashboard. These operate under a separate scope and process.
