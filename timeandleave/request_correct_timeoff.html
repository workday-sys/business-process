Interactive guide to Request Time Off and Correct/Update Time Off workflows — roles, steps, decisions, and approvals

Time & Leave at a Glance
3 Business Processes · City Positions Only · Federal Work Study & State Positions Excluded
📅
Request Time Off
Employee or HR/Admin initiates a time-off request. Routes to Manager/Timekeeper for approval; HR Partner approval only when an attachment is included. 2-day SLA.
✏️
Correct Time Off
Used when a time-off request has already been approved and needs a change. Triggers a separate correction business process with the same approval chain.
🔄
Update / Cancel Time Off
Used when a request is still in a submitted/pending state (not yet approved). Initiator can cancel or update directly. Broad group of initiators including Management Chain. 1-day SLA.
Key Concepts & Definitions
Important distinctions clarified during the session
💡
Time Off vs. Leave of AbsenceTime Off covers shorter, discrete absences (vacation, sick days, etc.). Leave of Absence is a separate, longer-duration process and is NOT covered in this dashboard.
💡
Correction vs. CancelA Cancel is used when the request is still in a submitted/pending state and has not been approved yet — the initiator can withdraw it directly. A Correction is used only after the business process has already been approved; it triggers a brand-new correction business process with a fresh approval chain.
⚠️
HR Partner Approval — Only When There Is an AttachmentHR Partner does NOT approve every time-off request. They are only pulled into the workflow when the request includes an attachment (e.g., a doctor's note for 3–5 days of sick leave).
🔀
Approval SLA — Informational, Not AutomaticThe 2-day (Request) and 1-day (Update) timeframes are targets communicated to approvers. The system does not auto-complete or auto-approve after the deadline.
🚫
Excluded from this DashboardFederal Work Study positions and State-specific steps have been excluded per your request. All flows shown apply to City positions only. Time Tracking and Leave of Absence are also out of scope for this Part 1 session.

Decision Flags
⚠️
Attachment = HR Partner StepIf the request includes an attachment (e.g., doctor's note), it routes to HR Partner for a second approval. Without an attachment, the process ends after Manager/Timekeeper approves.
🔀
Cancel vs. CorrectIf the request has NOT yet been approved, the initiator can simply cancel it. If it IS already approved, a separate Correction business process must be used.

Key Rules
⚠️
Correction ≠ CancelCorrection is a separate business process triggered only AFTER the original request is fully approved. It goes through the same Manager/Timekeeper and (if attachment) HR Partner approval chain again.
💡
Same Approval Logic AppliesThe correction business process mirrors the Request Time Off approval chain: Manager/Timekeeper first, then HR Partner only if an attachment is included.
🔀
Visible in the Action ItemsWhen a correction is submitted, it appears in the approver's action items labeled as "Absence Correction" (distinct from "Absence Request") so approvers can differentiate.

Update / Cancel Time Off

City positions · Used when a request is still in submitted/pending state (not yet approved)

SLA1 Day
Who Can Initiate
  • Employee Can update or cancel own pending request
  • Manager Can update or cancel
  • Management Chain Broader chain of managers can act
  • Timekeeper Can update or cancel
  • HR Partner Can update or cancel
  • HR Administrator Can update or cancel
  • HR Specialist Can update or cancel
  • Absence Administrator Can update or cancel
  • Absence Partner Can update or cancel
Key Rules
⚠️
Only for Pending (Not-Yet-Approved) RequestsThis process applies when the original request is still in a submitted/awaiting-approval state. Once approved, use the Correct Time Off process instead.
💡
Management Chain Unique to This ProcessUnlike Request and Correct Time Off, the Update process includes the broader Management Chain as initiators — giving supervisors up the reporting structure the ability to act on pending requests.
🔀
No Separate Approval Loop RequiredBecause the original request was never approved, updates or cancellations are processed with a 1-day SLA and do not re-trigger the full Manager/HR Partner approval chain for the cancellation itself.

Key Decision Points Summary
Conditions that change the workflow path
⚠️
Decision Point 1 — Is There an Attachment?Applies to both Request Time Off and Correct Time Off. If YES → HR Partner approval required as Step 2. If NO → process ends after Manager/Timekeeper approval. Attachments are typically required for extended sick leave (3–5+ days with a doctor's note).
⚠️
Decision Point 2 — Has the Request Already Been Approved?If the original request is still PENDING → use Update/Cancel Time Off (initiator can act directly). If the original request is already APPROVED → use Correct Time Off (triggers a new business process with full approval chain).
🔀
Role Access Determines VisibilityWhat a user sees in the system is role-dependent. Employees see only their own absence data. Absence Partners can look up other workers' balances. HR Partners need to confirm whether they also hold Absence Partner access to see those balances.
💡
Super Users & Proxy AccessSuper users with proxy access can complete approval tasks on behalf of another user. This is relevant for testing and for delegated approval scenarios.
