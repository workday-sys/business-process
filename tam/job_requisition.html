Job Requisition Processes at a Glance
4 Business Processes · City Positions · Excluding Federal Work Study & State Positions
📄
Create Job Requisition
Full lifecycle from initiation through review, approval, and posting. Includes conditional Budget Manager approval and CDO equity review.
📌
Post Job
Sub-process of Create Job Req — or standalone. Primary Recruiter / Civil Service Administrator posts internally and/or externally.
🔄
Update Job Posting
Un-post and re-post a job. Multiple roles can update the posting end date or modify details before reposting.
👤
Create Prospect
Add potential candidates before they apply. Initiated internally by HR Admin/Manager/Recruiter, or by the prospect themselves via the external career site.
Key Decision Points & Conditions
Flags to know before testing or using these processes
⚠️
Review Step Skipped When Self-InitiatedIf the HR Partner, Civil Service Director, or Faculty Relations Partner initiates the requisition themselves, their own review step is skipped — they already own the record.
🔀
Budget Manager Approval — Conditional on Pay RangeBudget Manager approval is triggered when proposed compensation falls outside the established salary range. If compensation is within range, that step is bypassed.
🔀
Population Determines Who PostsFor regular staff/faculty positions → Primary Recruiter posts. For Civil Service positions → Civil Service Administrator posts.
🆕
New in A&C2: Expanded Posting RolesPreviously only Primary Recruiters could post. Now Recruiters and Recruiting Administrators can also initiate and complete the post/un-post job task as a standalone process.
🔀
Manager Approval Required After CDO ReviewAfter the Chief Diversity Officer reviews the requisition, a final Manager approval step is required before the job is posted.
🚫
Federal Work Study & State Positions ExcludedAll workflows on this dashboard apply to City positions only. Federal Work Study and State-specific process steps are excluded throughout.

Completed UponSubmission

  • HR Administrator — Can create a prospect internally
  • Manager — Can create a prospect internally
  • Recruiter — Can create a prospect internally
  • Recruiting Administrator — Can create a prospect internally
  • External Prospect (Self) — "Introduce Yourself" via external career site
What is a Prospect?
💼
Like a Business CardA prospect is someone whose basic info is stored in the system but who has not yet applied to a specific job. They are not tied to a job requisition yet.
🔍
Find Candidates ReportProspects appear in the Find Candidates report alongside active candidates. Teams can filter by candidate type (Prospect). From there, prospects can be invited to apply or have a job application created on their behalf.

Key Decision Points & Flags
Conditions that change the process flow
⚠️
Self-Initiated = Review Step SkippedApplies to: Create Job Requisition. When HR Partner, Faculty Relations Partner, or Civil Service Director initiates the requisition themselves, their own review step is automatically bypassed.
🔀
Budget Manager Approval — Conditional on Pay RangeIn the Create Job Requisition process, if the proposed compensation for a full-time position is outside the established salary range, the Budget Manager approval step is triggered. If within range, it is skipped.
🔀
Posting Role Depends on PopulationPrimary Recruiter posts for regular staff and faculty positions. Civil Service Administrator posts for civil service positions.
🆕
New in A&C2 — Expanded Posting CapabilitiesPost Job and Un-Post Job can now be initiated and completed by Recruiters and Recruiting Administrators as standalone tasks, in addition to Primary Recruiters.
💬
Send Back Requires a CommentAt any review or approval step, if a reviewer sends the requisition back to a previous step (initiator or prior reviewer), a comment explaining the reason is mandatory.
🚫
Federal Work Study & State Positions ExcludedAll processes and roles described in this dashboard apply to City positions only. Federal Work Study and State-specific configurations are outside this scope.
