Requisition Management at a Glance
5 Business Processes · City Positions · Excluding Federal Work Study & State-Only Steps
🌲
Evergreen Requisition
Long-term candidate pool requisition. Must link to a job req before hiring. Completes after all reviews.
✏️
Change / Edit Requisition
Edit job description, time type, compensation, remote type, or assigned roles. Triggers unpost/repost if currently posted.
🔒
Close Job Requisition
Permanently close a requisition. Candidates must be dispositioned first. Cannot close same day as creation.
❄️
Freeze / Unfreeze
Temporarily pause recruiting. Auto-unposts on freeze. Repost required on unfreeze. Mass freeze available.
🔗
Duplicate Management
Identify and merge duplicate candidate records. Triggered when a potential duplicate is detected in the system.
Security Roles Legend
Who participates across all processes
🔵 HR Partner 🟣 Manager / Hiring Manager 🔴 Primary Recruiter / Recruiter 🟢 Chief Diversity Officer 🟠 Civil Service Admin / Director ⚫ HR Administrator / Recruiting Admin
⚠️
Key Rule: Evergreen Cannot Hire DirectlyCandidates applied to an Evergreen requisition must be moved to a linked job requisition (matching job profile) before an offer can be extended. Hiring from an Evergreen is not possible in Workday.
🔗
Evergreen → Job Requisition LinkThe connection between an Evergreen and a job requisition is established by a shared job profile. Both must have the same job profile to link.
❄️
Freeze vs UnfreezeFreeze and Unfreeze use the same task in Workday — the action is determined by the current state of the requisition. Freezing auto-unposts; unfreezing requires manual repost by Primary Recruiter or Civil Service Admin.
🚫
Excluded from this DashboardFederal Work Study Coordinator roles and State-specific steps have been excluded per scope definition. Contact your FWS or State team for those workflows.

Close Job Requisition

Permanently close a requisition. Automatic processes run on close: unpost job, optionally close position, decline remaining candidates.

Completes WhenAll Approvals Done
Who is Involved
  • HR Partner Can initiate · Approves
  • Chief Diversity Officer Can initiate
  • Primary Recruiter Can initiate
  • Faculty Relations Partner Can initiate
  • Civil Service Admin Can initiate
  • Civil Service Director Can initiate · Approves
Key Rules & Notes
🚫
Cannot Close Same Day as CreationEffective date is end of midnight, so closing must happen the following day. If created in error, simply do not post — candidates won't see it.
⚠️
Disposition Candidates FirstAll active candidates must be moved to a final disposition (declined or otherwise) before closing the requisition.
🔄
Automatic Processes on CloseWorkday automatically runs: Unpost Job · Optionally Close the linked Position · Decline Remaining Candidates.

Duplicate Management

Identify and merge duplicate candidate records. Triggered when Workday detects a potential match between a candidate profile and a worker record.

Triggered WhenDuplicate Detected
Who Can Merge Duplicates
  • Primary Recruiter Manually merge records
  • Recruiter Manually merge records
  • Recruiting Admin Manually merge records
  • HR Administrator Manually merge records
  • HR Partner Manually merge records
Reports Available
📊
Manage Duplicate RecordsView all flagged potential duplicates in the system.
📊
Manage Merged RecordsAudit trail of all previously merged candidate records.
Why Duplicates Happen
⚠️
External Application by Internal WorkerIf an existing CUNY employee accidentally applies via the external career site instead of the internal site, a new candidate record is created — separate from their worker record — creating a duplicate.
🔍
Workday Checks AutomaticallyWhen candidates come in, Workday checks for matching records. If a match is found, the system flags it for manual review and merge.
🚫
Do Not Leave UnmergedAn unmerged duplicate means the hiring team won't see the candidate's full worker history. Always merge before proceeding with hiring steps.

Decision Points & Conditions Summary
Flags across all 5 processes
⚠️
Evergreen: Cannot Hire DirectlyCandidates must always be moved to a linked job requisition (matching job profile) before an offer or hire can take place. Hiring directly from an Evergreen is not supported in Workday.
⚠️
Change Req: Unpost/Repost if Currently PostedAny edit to a live (posted) job requisition triggers an unpost → review → repost cycle. This ensures career site accuracy.
⚠️
Close Req: Cannot Close Same Day as CreationEffective date is end of midnight. Wait until the following day. If opened in error, do not post — just close the next day.
🔀
Civil Service Routing ConditionFor civil service job requisitions, the Civil Service Director participates in review/approval. For non-civil-service reqs, those steps are skipped. Civil Service Admin handles the Post Job step for civil service reqs.
❄️
Freeze: Auto-UnpostWhen a requisition is frozen, Workday automatically unposts it. No manual unpost step is needed. When unfreezing, the repost must be done manually by Primary Recruiter or Civil Service Admin.
📦
Mass Freeze OptionIf an entire campus or college needs a hiring freeze, the Mass Freeze/Unfreeze task is available — no need to freeze each requisition individually.
🔗
Evergreen Lifecycle Best PracticeClose and reopen Evergreen requisitions each semester. Keeping them open indefinitely leads to stale candidate pools and potential compliance issues. Best practice is to post for a defined period, unpost, and reopen as needed.
🚫
Excluded: FWS & StateFederal Work Study Coordinator roles and all State-specific process steps are excluded from this dashboard per scope definition.
