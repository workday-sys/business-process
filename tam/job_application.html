Job Application Process at a Glance
7 Active Recruitment Stages · City Positions · Excluding Federal Work Study & State Positions
📥
Apply
Internal & external candidates submit applications via career sites. Always the first step.
🔍
Review
All candidates land here first. Recruiter reviews qualifications, resumes, and questionnaire responses.
📞
Screen
Optional phone or screening interview stage. Primary Recruiter manages questionnaire and decision.
🎤
Interview
Formal interview stage. Multiple rounds possible. Search Committee and Hiring Manager involved.
📋
Reference Check
Candidate adds references. Primary Recruiter manages follow-up and makes the decision.
📄
Offer
Formal offer lifecycle: initiation, HR Partner review, Budget Manager approval, candidate acknowledgment.
🔒
Background Check
Primary Recruiter selects background check status and makes the final background check decision.
✅
Ready for Hire
Final stage. Primary Recruiter moves candidate; HR Partner initiates the applicable hiring process.
Security Roles Legend
Who participates across all recruitment stages
🟢 Primary Recruiter 🔵 HR Partner 🟡 Budget Manager 🔴 Hiring Manager 🟣 Candidate (Internal / External) ⚫ Recruiting Administrator
📌
Stages Are Not Always SequentialThe recruitment flow in Workday is dynamic. Candidates can move between stages in different orders. Screen, Interview, Reference Check, and Background Check are all conditional — not every candidate goes through every stage.
⚠️
Review Stage Behaves DifferentlyUnlike other stages, Review tasks do NOT appear in user inboxes because so many candidates may apply at once. Recruiters must actively navigate to the candidate pipeline to see candidates in Review status.
🔀
Knockout Questions Can Auto-DispositionIf a candidate answers a knockout question in a way that disqualifies them (e.g., indicating they live internationally), they are automatically "dispositioned" (declined) in the system without manual review.
🎤
Interview Scheduling — Shared TaskWhen multiple roles can schedule an interview (Primary Recruiter, Search Committee Member/Chair, Hiring Manager), the task goes to all their inboxes simultaneously. The first person to act on it removes it from everyone else's inbox.
🚫
Excluded from this DashboardFederal Work Study positions and State-specific steps have been excluded per your request. Assessment stage is also excluded as it is NOT being used. All flows shown apply to City positions only.

Key Decision Points & Conditions
Flags to know before testing or using these processes
⚠️
Review Does Not Use the InboxReview is the only stage where tasks do NOT appear in user inboxes. Because so many candidates can apply at once, recruiters must navigate directly to the requisition pipeline to see and act on Review-stage candidates.
⚠️
Offer — Candidate Decline Sends Task BackIf a candidate declines the offer, the Primary Recruiter receives a "Send Back" task. They can re-route the process to any prior offer step: initiation, HR approval, Budget approval, or document generation.
🔀
Stage Flow is DynamicCandidates do not need to visit every stage. Screen, Interview, Reference Check, and Background Check are all optional. The order can also be adjusted based on institutional practice. The only mandatory transitions are: Apply → Review, and successful offer → Ready for Hire.
🔀
Interview Scheduling — Shared Task, First-Come Removes ItWhen multiple roles (Primary Recruiter, Search Committee Chair/Member, Hiring Manager) can all schedule an interview, the task appears in all their inboxes simultaneously. Whoever acts on it first removes it from all other inboxes.
💡
Administrative Support for Interview SchedulingIf admin support staff need to schedule interviews on behalf of a search committee (without being actual committee members), they should be added as a Search Committee Member in the system to access the scheduling task. This is logged as a configuration item for follow-up.
💡
Reference Check Task — Pending Configuration UpdateCurrent setup routes the reference management task to the Primary Recruiter. Session feedback indicated this may better belong with the Hiring Manager, who typically handles reference checks after interviews. This is flagged as a defect for review.
🚫
Assessment Stage — NOT BEING USEDWhile Workday supports an Assessment stage, this has been confirmed as not in use. Candidates skip from Review/Screen directly to Interview.
🚫
Excluded: Federal Work Study & State PositionsFederal Work Study positions and State-specific recruitment steps have been excluded from this dashboard per your request. All flows apply to City positions only.
