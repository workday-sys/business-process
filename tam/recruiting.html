Faculty & Staff Recruiting at a Glance
5 Business Processes · City Positions · Excluding Federal Work Study & State Positions
📌
Create Position
Establish a new position before recruiting begins. Initiated by HR Partner or Manager; finalized by Budget Manager. Ad hoc — most positions are pre-loaded annually.
📝
Create Job Requisition
Kick off the recruiting pipeline. Multiple roles can initiate. Reviewed by HR, Faculty Relations & Chief Diversity Officer, approved by Budget Manager, then posted by the Primary Recruiter.
👤
Manage Candidates
Review is required first; Screen, Interview, References, and Background Check are flexible. A make-decision step ends every stage — move forward or disposition.
📄
Offer
Primary Recruiter generates the offer letter. Routes to HR Partner (always) and Budget Manager (only if comp differs from requisition). Candidate accepts or declines.
✅
Ready for Hire
Final handoff from Recruiting to HCM. Primary Recruiter triggers; HR Partner initiates Hire, Change Job, or Add Job depending on candidate type.

Create Job Requisition

Start of the recruiting process · Includes initiation, review, approval, and job posting sub-process · Budget now reviews all non-FWS positions

Completes WhenPrimary Recruiter Posts Job
Who is Involved
  • Manager Can initiate; must assign Primary Recruiter; provides final approval
  • HR Partner Can initiate; reviews (if not initiator)
  • Faculty Relations Partner Can initiate (faculty & college lab tech); reviews (if not initiator)
  • Chief Diversity Officer Always reviews — regardless of who initiates
  • Budget Manager Approves requisition
  • Primary Recruiter Can initiate (faculty & lab tech); posts the job (must be assigned — required)
  • Recruiter Can initiate for faculty & college lab tech positions
Decision Flags

🔀
Recruiting Instructions Guide PostingThe initiator sets instructions (internal only, external only, internal & external, or no posting required). These guide the Primary Recruiter when they execute the Post Job task.
🆕
ANC1 → ANC2 ChangeBudget Manager now reviews all positions (except FWS). Recruiters can now initiate job requisitions for faculty and college lab tech — no longer exclusive to Faculty Relations Partner.

Manage Candidates

Flexible pipeline · Review is mandatory first stage · Screen, Interview, References & Background Check can be done in any order · Make-decision step ends every stage

Completes WhenCandidate Moves to Offer or is Dispositioned
Who is Involved
  • Primary Recruiter Reviews, screens, schedules interviews, manages references, initiates background check, makes all stage decisions (disposition or move forward)
  • Manager / Interview Team Provides interview feedback (rating 1–5 scale)
  • HR Partner Can view background check results (restricted access)
  • Candidate Submits application; adds references; reviews offer; can withdraw at any time
Decision Flags
⚠️
Make Decision at Every StageAt the end of every stage, the Primary Recruiter decides: move forward to the next stage OR disposition (decline) the candidate. Review is the only mandatory first stage.
🔀
Internal vs External CandidatesExternal candidates apply via the external career site. Internal candidates (current employees) apply via the internal career site. Both land in the Review stage first.
🔀
Background Check via TrueView IntegrationPrimary Recruiter selects a background check package. TrueView (integrated with Workday) contacts the candidate directly. Results (Pass / Fail / Pending) feed back into Workday for the recruiter's make-decision step.
🔀
References via External LinkReferences do not log into Workday. They receive an external link to submit their reference letter, which then populates on the candidate's profile in Workday.

Offer

Primary Recruiter initiates · Offer details, comp review, offer letter generation, HR Partner approval, optional Budget Manager approval, candidate acceptance or decline

Completes WhenCandidate Accepts Offer
Who is Involved
  • Primary Recruiter Initiates; enters hire date & offer details; optional sign-on bonus; generates offer letter; makes offer decision
  • HR Partner Approves offer (if not the initiator)
  • Budget Manager Approves only if proposed comp differs from what was on the requisition
  • Candidate Reviews and accepts or declines the offer letter (must provide a reason if declining)
Decision Flags
⚠️
Budget Manager Approval is ConditionalBudget Manager only receives an approval task if the proposed compensation amount is DIFFERENT from what was stated on the job requisition.
🔀
Candidate Declines — Send Back TaskIf a candidate declines, they must enter a reason. Primary Recruiter receives a send-back task to review the comment and decide: re-offer, regenerate the letter, or disposition the candidate.
🔀
Sign-On Bonus OptionA one-time sign-on bonus can be added to compensation details before the offer letter is generated.

Ready for Hire

Final stage of recruiting · Handoff from Recruiting to HCM · Primary Recruiter triggers; HR Partner initiates the appropriate hiring action in Workday HCM

Completes WhenHR Partner Completes Hiring Action in HCM
Who is Involved
  • Primary Recruiter Moves candidate to Ready for Hire after offer acceptance & background check passed
  • HR Partner Receives task automatically in inbox; initiates the applicable hiring action in HCM
Decision Flags
⚠️
Internal vs External Determines Hiring ActionExternal candidate (brand new hire) → HR Partner initiates a Hire action. Internal candidate (current employee) → HR Partner initiates either a Change Job (changing roles) or Add Job (adding a concurrent position).
🔀
The Handshake Between Recruiting & HCMThis is the transition point where recruiting ends and HCM onboarding begins. The task lands automatically in the HR Partner's inbox once the Primary Recruiter completes the Ready for Hire step.

Key Decision Points & Conditions
Flags to know before testing or using these processes
⚠️
Self-Initiated = Review Skipped (Create Job Requisition)When HR Partner or Faculty Relations Partner initiates the job requisition, they bypass their own review step because they already authored the request. Chief Diversity Officer always reviews regardless.
⚠️
Self-Initiated = Review Skipped (Create Position)When HR Partner initiates the Create Position process, the subsequent review step is skipped. Any other initiating role triggers the HR Partner review.
🔀
Full-Time vs Part-Time — Create PositionFull-time positions: Budget Manager adds budget data and provides final approval. Part-time positions: Manager adds budget data instead and that action completes the process.
🔀
Offer Comp Variance Triggers Budget ManagerBudget Manager is only pulled into the Offer process if the proposed compensation differs from the amount on the job requisition. Same amount = no Budget Manager step.
🔀
Internal Candidate Hiring Action Depends on Role StatusInternal candidates hired via recruiting result in either a Change Job (if they are moving into a new primary role) or an Add Job (if they are adding a concurrent appointment) in HCM.
🆕
ANC1 → ANC2 ChangesBudget Manager now approves all job requisitions (except FWS). Recruiters can now initiate job requisitions for faculty and college lab tech positions — previously exclusive to Faculty Relations Partner.
🚫
Primary Recruiter Must Be Assigned on RequisitionSubmitting a job requisition without an assigned Primary Recruiter returns an error. The Primary Recruiter is a required field before submission.
🗑️
Excluded: Federal Work Study & State-Specific StepsFWS Coordinator roles, FWS career site references, and State-funded position workflows have been removed from this dashboard per your request. All flows apply to City positions only.
