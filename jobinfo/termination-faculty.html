Voluntary vs. Involuntary Drives the Whole FlowManager Approval is skipped entirely when the termination reason is involuntary. Managers currently only see voluntary termination reasons in the system — involuntary reason codes are not available to the Manager role.
🔀
Single Job vs. Multiple JobsIf the worker has more than one active job, Termination cannot complete until every job is ended. This adds a full End Additional Job loop (HR Partner → Payroll Partner → Manager → Payroll Partner) before the process can finish.

Notes & Clarifications from the Review Session
Questions, concerns, and open items raised by campus testers during the demo — captured for quick reference
🗣️
Manager Approval Adds Delay (Voluntary Resignations)Testers pointed out that the Manager isn't approving anything substantive when an employee simply resigns, and that adding a manager step to an already multi-step process risks delaying HR work. Recommended for reconsideration; may be logged as a defect.
🚫
Repeated Payroll Data Entry for Additional Jobs (Known Defect)When a worker has an additional job, Payroll Partner has to re-enter payroll data a second time through the End Additional Job loop. This is already logged as a known defect; the team is waiting on feedback about whether to remove the duplicate entry.
⚠️
HR Partner Approving Their Own InitiationWhen an HR Partner initiates the termination and later has to complete an End Additional Job task, that appears as a second approval step for the same person. This is expected system behavior when an additional job exists — not avoidable given current configuration.
🔀
Ending One Job vs. Full TerminationIf a worker is only leaving one of several jobs (e.g. leaving a full-time role but keeping an adjunct position elsewhere), that is an End Additional Job action, not a Termination — Termination is reserved for a worker leaving the organization entirely.
🚫
No Instant "Kill Switch" for Urgent SituationsThere is no way to skip configured steps to immediately terminate and shut down all access in an emergency (e.g. an escort-off-campus scenario). The recommended approach is a manual/offline step (a phone call to immediately cut off access) done in parallel with — not in place of — the Workday process, since downstream deprovisioning is tied to process completion.
🚫
Cross-Campus Job Visibility Is LimitedHR Partners currently cannot see a worker's positions at other campuses, which makes it hard to know upfront whether a "Termination" or an "End Additional Job" is the correct action. The security/visibility model for this is still being worked out with the central security team; testers were encouraged to log this as a defect if it affects their campus.
🔀
Adjuncts Across Multiple CampusesIf an adjunct works at more than one campus and the initiator doesn't know about the other position, starting a Termination could be the wrong action. Checking the worker's profile for the "additional jobs" toggle before proceeding is recommended; the system will also surface this if the End Additional Job step is reached mid-process.
ℹ️
Retiree / Benefits Example Not Demoed LiveA live example of a retirement-reason termination with active benefits (COBRA, benefit reinstatement, etc.) was not shown, since Benefits testing readiness was uncertain at the time and the sample worker had no benefits on file. Confirmed: benefits handling (Change Benefit Elections) is part of this same business process, not a separate one.
🚫
Overnight Feed Defaulting Jobs to "Human Resources" DepartmentA concern was raised that data feeds may default all incoming non-employee/POI jobs to a "Human Resources" department regardless of actual assignment, since a clean report wasn't yet available on the source side. Recommended to be logged as a defect flagging the department-assignment issue for review against the data extracts.
ℹ️
Statuses Referenced in TestingDraft = started but not submitted. In Progress = submitted but still routing through approvals. A Defect = an error or unintended action found during testing. A Fail = the test outcome did not match what was expected. A Pass = the outcome matched the user story / test expectation.
🗣️
Testing Logistics (for reference)Central testers, campus proxy super users, and campus testers each have different levels of system access. If you're a campus tester without proxy access, steps that land with another role (e.g. Payroll Partner) will need to be moved along by the test facilitator on your behalf. Anything that looks missing on screen may be a security-role gap — use the Security Request Form to flag it, and the Defect Form to log unexpected behavior.
