Scholarships & Honorariums at a Glance
4 Core Business Processes · Excluding Federal Work Study & State-Only Steps
🏛️
Create Position
Budget Manager, HR Administrator, HR Partner, or Manager creates a Non-Employee position tagged Scholarship Recipient or Honorarium.
💼
Add Additional Job
HR Partner adds a second, fixed-term job to an existing employee so they can receive the payment without disturbing their primary record.
🤝
Hire / Convert Employee
HR Partner hires a brand-new non-worker, or converts an existing Contingent Worker, into a Non-Employee record to receive the payment.
💰
One-Time Payment
Budget Manager, Payroll Partner, and Comp Partner approve the actual scholarship or honorarium dollar amount.
Security Roles Legend
Who participates across all Scholarship & Honorarium processes
🔵 HR Partner ⚪ HR Administrator 🟠 HR Specialist 🟣 Manager 🟡 Budget Manager 🔷 Payroll Administrator 🌊 Payroll Partner 🟤 Comp Partner 🟢 Recruiter / Recruiting Administrator 🔴 Non-Employee (Recipient)
⚠️
Which Process Do I Use? Depends on the Recipient's Current StatusAlready an active employee → use Add Additional Job. Not a worker at all today → use Hire as a Non-Employee. Currently a Contingent Worker or POI → must be converted to an employee with the Non-Employee subtype first, following the same steps as a new hire.
🔀
Scholarship vs. Honorarium — Same Process, Different Job ProfileBoth use identical position, hire, and payment steps. The only difference is the job profile selected (Scholarship Recipient vs. Honorarium/Visitor Honorarium), which drives the title and tagging used for reporting.
ℹ️
Only Two Campuses Process Scholarship Recipients TodayBased on recent feedback, only the Graduate Center and Brooklyn College currently process actions for Scholarship Recipients. Separate supervisory organizations have been created at those two campuses to hold and manage these recipients.
🔀
Non-Employee vs. Contingent WorkerContingent Workers are paid through outside agencies, not through Payroll — their records show "CW" next to their name and carry less data than an employee record. Because Contingent Workers cannot receive a one-time payment directly, anyone receiving a scholarship or honorarium must be tracked using the Non-Employee employee type, regardless of their prior worker status.
📅
When the Payment EndsOnce the scholarship or honorarium has been paid and the recipient no longer needs system access, it's recommended to terminate the Non-Employee record so they don't remain active without need.
🚫
Excluded from this DashboardFederal Work Study roles, steps, and eligibility questions, along with State-specific positions and validations, have been excluded. These operate under a separate scope.

Decision Flags
⚠️
Full-Time vs. Part-Time — Overload ValidationAn employee cannot hold two full-time jobs. If they already have a full-time job, a hard error fires requiring a supporting document attachment before the additional job can proceed.
🔀
Fixed-Term End DateThe additional job is entered as fixed-term with an end employment date, so records can be cleaned up once the payment period ends.

Role Definitions
Security role descriptions for Scholarships & Honorariums scope
Role Description Can Wear Other Hats?
HR Partner The primary driver of these processes — initiates positions, adds jobs, hires and converts workers, enters personal information and government IDs, and initiates the one-time payment. Usually the role that reviews and completes Create Position. ✔ Can also be Manager
HR Administrator Can initiate Create Position and supports Add Additional Job and Hire/Convert steps. Broader administrative role. ✔ Can hold other HR roles
HR Specialist Supports entry on Add Additional Job and Hire/Convert Employee transactions. ✔ Can also be HR Partner
Manager Can initiate Create Position, Add Additional Job, and Hire/Convert transactions for their area. ✔ Can also be HR Partner
Budget Manager Can initiate Create Position and gives consolidated approval on Add Additional Job. Approves the transaction inside the One-Time Payment sub-process. ✔ Can hold other approval roles
Payroll Administrator Automatically assigns the pay group during Add Additional Job and Hire/Convert transactions. Not a manual approval step. –
Payroll Partner Gives final/consolidated approval on Add Additional Job, Hire/Convert Employee, and the One-Time Payment sub-process. Also edits Other IDs on hire. The role that most often finalizes these transactions. ✔ Central-only role — not available to proxy into
Comp Partner Approves the One-Time Payment amount before the final Payroll Partner sign-off. –
Recruiter / Recruiting Administrator Support roles within the Hire/Convert Employee process, primarily for the pre-hire and hire steps. ✔ Can hold other HR roles
Non-Employee (Recipient) The scholarship or honorarium recipient's record. Always tagged with the Non-Employee employee type and a Scholarship/Honorarium job profile, regardless of whether they were previously an employee, a non-worker, or a Contingent Worker. Recommended for termination once payment is complete. N/A
🚫
Excluded: Federal Work Study & State PositionsFWS-specific roles, steps, and eligibility questions, along with State-specific positions and validations, are not included in this dashboard. These operate under a separate scope and process.
ℹ️
Payroll Partner is Central-OnlyPayroll Partner roles sit with the Central team only — campus users are not able to proxy into that role. Only the assigned Payroll Partner can complete those approval steps.
