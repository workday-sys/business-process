Change Job — Staff & Faculty at a Glance
1 Core Business Process · 3 Phases · City Positions · Excluding Federal Work Study & State-Only Steps
🚀
Part 1: Initiation & Setup
HR Partner/Administrator/Specialist or Manager initiates. Manager reviews (if a transfer), org assignments, compensation, pay group, and position/payroll data are set.
✅
Part 2: Approvals
Budget Manager, Manager's Manager, HR Partner, and Compensation Partner give consolidated approvals as applicable, then Payroll Partner gives the final approval.
🔄
Part 3: Post-Completion
Automated accrual adjustment, time off updates, benefit elections, academic appointment updates, and other steps fire after approval — conditional on the scenario.
🔁
Related Processes
Request Transfer (a trigger task), Edit Position (a separate process for vacant positions), Service Dates Change, and Change Contingent Worker Details.
Security Roles Legend
Who participates across the Change Job process
🔵 HR Partner / Administrator / Specialist 🟣 Manager (incl. Manager's Manager) 🟡 Budget Manager 🌊 Payroll Administrator / Partner 🔷 Compensation Partner ⚪ Security / HR Org / Absence Administrator 🌸 Academic Faculty Partner 🔴 Employee (Self) ⚙️ Workday (System / Automated)
ℹ️
Initiators, Approvers, Reviewers & To-DosInitiators start the business process. Approvers/Reviewers can approve or deny; Reviewers can also modify the transaction. To-Do steps let a role perform an additional task mid-process (e.g. Security Administrator's role/group review). A submission left un-submitted stays in Draft; once submitted it is In Progress until approved or denied.
⚠️
Initiation Method Changes the PathChange Job can be initiated directly, via the Request Transfer task (adds Current/Receiving Manager review steps), or triggered alongside Change Contingent Worker Details (skips the Propose Compensation Change step, since compensation is handled there instead).
🔀
Full-Time vs. Part-Time Drives Several StepsPart-time positions route to the HR Partner for position data (appointment/professional hours) and to the Budget Manager for a manual review. Full-time positions route to an (usually automated) budget check instead.
🚫
Excluded: Federal Work Study & State PositionsFWS positions and State-specific steps (e.g. Senior College "State Payroll Data") are not included in this dashboard. Only the City / Community College payroll path is shown. These operate under a separate scope.
🛠️
Open Items Still Being Refined with Central TeamRaised during the walkthrough and not yet resolved: (1) adjunct workload waivers aren't yet built into the Budget Manager's review step, (2) hiring-fee override/exemption rules for Civil Service classified titles are still being defined, (3) full-time faculty workload/contact-hour tracking is a separate, ongoing requirements conversation, and (4) several campuses flagged concerns that the approval sequence (e.g. routing back to HR Partner after Manager approval, or requiring Manager's Manager sign-off) could create delays for large batches of promotions — logged for central review, not yet changed.
🚀

📅
Not Every Step Applies Every TimeThis is a long, encompassing business process — a title-only change will skip most of the conditional steps above, while a transfer with a promotion and compensation change can trigger nearly all of them.
💬
Q&A Clarification — Change Job vs. Position Management vs. Edit PositionChange Job is for positions someone currently occupies. Editing attributes of an unfilled position uses the separate Edit Position process instead (see Related Processes tab). Position Management (changing position attributes directly) is a different path than Change Job for filled positions.
💬
Q&A Clarification — Promotions Don't Always Need a Vacant PositionIf a person is being promoted in place (e.g. Assistant → Associate Professor) rather than moving into a different, already-approved vacant position, choose a new Job Profile with reason "Promotion" rather than searching for an empty target position. Confirmed live during testing: changing the Job Profile does update the position's job title to match — it does not keep the old title.

Consolidated Approvals

Approved by Budget Manager · Manager's Manager · HR Partner · Compensation Partner · Payroll Partner  |  Part 2 of 3

Completes WhenApproved by Payroll Partner
Who is Involved
  • Budget Manager Consolidated approval — part-time positions only
  • Manager's Manager Consolidated approval — promotion or compensation change
  • HR Partner Consolidated approval (if not the initiator); also edits service dates (optional)
  • Compensation Partner Consolidated approval — applicable job classifications
  • Payroll Partner Final consolidated approval — required every time
  • Security Administrator To-Do: reviews/assigns security roles if applicable
Decision Flags
⚠️
Four Conditional Approvals, One Required OneBudget Manager, Manager's Manager, HR Partner, and Compensation Partner approvals are all conditional. Payroll Partner's final consolidated approval is the only one required on every Change Job — it's what actually completes the process.
🚫
Feedback Logged — Approval Sequence ConcernsMultiple campuses raised concerns that routing back to the HR Partner after Manager approval, and requiring Manager's Manager sign-off for promotions, could create bottlenecks (especially for department chairs handling many faculty promotions at once, or where a Manager's Manager has no system access). Logged for central-team review — not yet changed.

Post-Completion Steps

Triggered automatically once Payroll Partner approves  |  Part 3 of 3

TimingOccur After Completion
Who is Involved
  • Workday (System) Automated accrual adjustment
  • Absence Administrator / Partner Time off updates & balance transfers
  • HR Organization Administrator Subordinate/superior org changes
  • Employee (Self) Benefit elections; hiring fee document review
  • Academic Faculty Partner Academic appointment updates

Decision Flags
🔀
Every Step Here Is ConditionalNone of these steps are guaranteed — during the live walkthrough, most showed "not required" for the specific worker being tested. Expect a different mix of steps depending on who and what you're testing.
ℹ️
Anything Left in the Worker's InboxIf the worker had other in-flight business processes when this Change Job completed, those are surfaced to the HR Partner to cancel or reassign — not automatically resolved.

🚫
Open Item — Hiring Fee Overrides & WaiversNot every classified-title hire owes the processing fee (e.g. some were already paid, or depend on how the person was hired). There's currently no way for HR to override this step from within Workday — exemption rules are still being worked out with the Civil Service team.
🛠️
Open Item — Adjunct Workload WaiversAdjuncts occasionally receive a waiver to teach beyond the standard contract limit. That waiver isn't yet built into the Budget Manager's compensation/workload review — flagged as a defect for the central budget team to address.
🛠️
Open Item — Full-Time Faculty Workload TrackingOnly part-time appointment/professional hours are tracked today (via the custom object shown in Part 1). Full-time contact-hour workload (often tracked in a campus Provost's-office "shadow system") is a separate, ongoing requirements conversation with the central policy team — not yet in scope.
Request Transfer (Trigger Task)
Not a separate approval chain — an initiation method that feeds into Change Job
Who is Involved
  • HR Partner Can initiate on behalf of a worker
  • Manager Can initiate; Current/Receiving Manager review
What It Changes
⚠️
Adds the Manager Review StepsChoosing "Request Transfer" as the reason is what turns on the Current Manager / Receiving Manager review steps at the very start of Change Job Part 1 — moving an employee from one manager/org to another.
Edit Position (Related but Separate Process)
Used only for positions nobody currently occupies
ℹ️
Don't Confuse the TwoChange Job is the main business process for changing a position someone currently fills. Editing attributes of an unfilled/vacant position uses the Edit Position (Restrictions) business process instead — it has far fewer approval steps and is used much less often. If a scenario seems like it should qualify for Edit Position instead of the full Change Job chain (e.g. a simple business-title-only change), log it as a defect for further discussion.
Service Dates Change (Standalone Sub-Process)
Ad hoc correction — does not require a full Change Job
Who is Involved
  • HR Partner Initiates ad hoc; approves if not the initiator
Why You'd Use It
🔀
Correcting Accrual Start DatesUseful when a transferred-in record shows the wrong service start date (e.g. shows an original hire date instead of a later rehire date), which would otherwise miscalculate accruals going forward. HR Partners can correct this directly, any time, without waiting on a full Change Job.

Role Definitions
Security role descriptions for Change Job — Staff & Faculty scope
Role Description Can Wear Other Hats?
HR Partner / Administrator / Specialist The most frequent initiator and actor across Change Job — starts the process, changes organization assignments, proposes compensation, updates part-time position data, edits service dates, and manages leftover business processes for the worker. ✔ Distinct HR Partner cannot approve a step they initiated
Manager (incl. Current / Receiving / Manager's Manager) Reviews transfer-related changes (Current/Receiving Manager) and gives consolidated approval for promotions or compensation changes (Manager's Manager). Not involved for changes that aren't transfers, promotions, or compensation-related. ✔ A Manager's Manager may not have regular Workday access — flagged as a training/change-management concern
Budget Manager Gives consolidated approval on part-time positions, reviewing compensation and appointment hours. Full-time positions instead go through an automated budget check with Payroll Partner. –
Payroll Administrator / Partner Assigns pay group (Administrator, if Primary Job); updates City payroll custom object data, runs/checks the budget for full-time positions, and gives the final consolidated approval that completes Change Job (Partner). Centralized — a bottleneck risk campuses have flagged. –
Compensation Partner Gives consolidated approval when the position has an applicable job classification for compensation review. –
Security Administrator To-Do steps only — reviews user-based security group assignments and/or assigns/changes role-based security assignments, when a position change affects a worker who already has those assignments. –
Absence Administrator / Partner Updates existing Time Off Requests (Administrator) and maintains Time Off Plan Transfer Balances when eligibility changes (Partner) — both conditional, post-completion steps. –
HR Organization Administrator Creates a subordinate organization if the worker becomes a new manager, and changes the superior organization if "Move Manager's Team" was selected — both post-completion steps. –
Academic Faculty Partner Updates the Academic Appointment record — only applies to part-time adjunct positions that already have a current academic appointment. –
Employee (Self) Changes their own benefit elections if the Change Job affects benefits, and completes the Pay Hiring Fee document-review step for Classified/Civil Service titles (when applicable — override/waiver rules still TBD). –
Workday (System / Automated) Runs the automated accrual adjustment after completion, and (usually) auto-checks budget for full-time positions before a manual Payroll Partner fallback is needed. –
🚫
Excluded: Federal Work Study & State PositionsFWS positions and State-specific steps (Senior College "State Payroll Data") are not included in this dashboard. All flows shown apply to City / Community College positions only.
