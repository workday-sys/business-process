Interactive guide to all 3 Additional Job workflows — roles, steps, decisions, and approvals. Built from the A&C2 configuration review sessions (March 19th, 2026) and the Add/End Additional Job walkthrough recording.

Additional Job Processes at a Glance
3 Business Processes · Faculty & Staff · City Positions · Excluding Federal Work Study & State-Only Steps
➕
Add Additional Job
Full lifecycle from initiation to triple Consolidated Approval. Includes org assignment, compensation, budget & payroll data.
🔄
Switch Primary Job
Designates which job is primary. Sub-process of Add Additional Job — routes for review only if HR Partner didn't initiate.
🛑
End Additional Job
Ends a secondary position. Critical validation, payroll update, Manager & Payroll Partner approval, closing Switch Primary Job step.
Security Roles Legend
Who participates across all three processes
🔵 HR Partner 🟡 Budget Manager 🟢 Payroll Partner 🟣 Manager ⚫ HR Administrator / HR Specialist 🔴 Academic Faculty Partner ⚫ Security Administrator
⚠️
Key Decision PointWho initiates the process changes which review/approval steps are required. If the HR Partner self-initiates a step like Switch Primary Job, they skip their own review. If another role initiates, the HR Partner must review first.
🔀
Full-Time vs Part-TimeIn Add Additional Job, Check Budget only runs for Full-Time proposed positions. Primary jobs are typically (but not required to be) full-time.
🚫
Excluded from this DashboardFederal Work Study positions and State-specific payroll steps have been excluded per your request. All flows shown apply to City positions only.

Staff & Faculty · Sub-process of Add Additional Job · Initiated by HR Administrator, HR Specialist, or Manager

Completes WhenHR Partner Reviews (or Immediately, if Self-Initiated)
Who is Involved

  • Manager Can initiate ad hoc (e.g. after the fact)
Decision Flags

Self-Initiated?If the HR Partner initiates → no separate review is required. If another role initiates → HR Partner must review and approve.

Only Needed When 2+ Jobs RemainIf exactly one job remains after a change, the system sets it as primary automatically — no manual step needed.
💬
Clarified in Q&AA primary job does not have to be full-time — technically possible, if uncommon, for a part-time position to be primary.

End Additional Job

Faculty & Staff · City positions · Initiated by HR Administrator or HR Specialist

Completes WhenInitiator Submits the Switch Primary Job Step
Who is Involved
  • HR Partner Reviews, assigns roles post-completion
  • Payroll Partner Updates city payroll data, approval
  • Manager Approval (flagged, see below)
  • Academic Faculty Partner Ends academic appointment (post-completion)
  • Security Administrator Reviews security groups (post-completion)
  • HR Administrator / HR Specialist Initiates
Decision Flags
🛑
Critical ValidationIf a Manager attempts to initiate End Additional Job for a Student Employee, the system blocks the submission.
💬
Open Discussion — Manager ApprovalHR flagged that Manager Approval (step 4) adds delay since offboarding is typically finalized outside the system first. Logged as a discussion item / potential defect — not yet removed as of the session date.
🔢
Switch Primary Job DependencyIf the ending job is the only other position, the remaining job becomes primary automatically. If 2+ jobs remain, the initiator must manually decide.

Key Decision Points & Conditions
Flags to know before testing or using these processes
⚠️
Self-Initiated = Review SkippedApplies to Switch Primary Job and, contextually, to review steps across the family. When the HR Partner initiates, they bypass their own review since they already know the request.
🔀
Full-Time vs Part-Time — Add Additional JobCheck Budget only runs for proposed Full-Time positions and is skipped for Non-Employee types. Confirmed a primary job does not strictly have to be full-time, though it typically is.
🎓
Student Employee SkipsJob Family = Student triggers multiple step skips across all three processes: org assignments, compensation, payroll data, manager approval, and switch primary job.
🔢
Switch Primary Job Only When 2+ Jobs RemainIf only one job remains after an add or end action, the system sets it as primary automatically — no manual switch needed.
💬
Open Discussion — Manager Approval in End Additional JobHR raised that this step adds delay since offboarding is typically finalized outside the system before initiation. Logged as a discussion item / potential defect — not yet confirmed as removed.
🛑
Critical Validation — End Additional JobThe system blocks a Manager from initiating an end action for a Student Employee's job.
🏢
City vs. Community College ConditionThe End Additional Job payroll data update step only applies when Job Family ≠ Student, Employee Type ≠ Non-Employee, and Company = Community College.
🚫
Excluded: Federal Work Study & State-Specific StepsFWS references and State Payroll Data / Senior College steps have been removed from this dashboard per your request. Contact your FWS team or State HR lead for those workflows.
🚫
Scope — City Positions OnlyAll processes and workflows shown across this dashboard apply to City positions only. Federal Work Study (FWS) positions and State-specific payroll steps are excluded from every guide. Contact your FWS team or State HR lead for those workflows.
