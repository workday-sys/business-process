Who participates across the Hire/Rehire process
🔵 HR Partner ⚪ HR Administrator ⚪ HR Specialist 🔷 Recruiter 🔷 Recruiting Administrator 🟣 Manager 🌊 Payroll Partner 🌊 Payroll Administrator 🟡 Budget Manager 🌸 Compensation Partner 🟤 Academic Faculty Partner 🔴 Employee ⚙️ Workday Service (System)
ℹ️
Multiple Roles Per PersonA person can wear multiple hats — an HR Partner could also be a Compensation Partner or Budget Manager elsewhere. Roles are assigned per organization, not exclusive.
⚠️
Direct Hire vs. Recruiting-Sourced HireA Direct Hire requires the HR Partner to create a Pre-Hire record and start a background check. A hire sourced through Recruiting arrives in the HR Partner's inbox automatically once a candidate is marked "Ready for Hire," and the background check step is skipped since it was already completed.
🔀
Full-Time vs. Part-Time — Different Approval PathsFull-time positions route through a Payroll Partner budget check. Part-time (e.g. adjunct) positions skip that check and instead route to a Budget Manager for consolidated approval, who also reviews appointment/professional hours.
🔀
Self-Initiated vs. Initiated by AnotherIf the HR Partner or Compensation Partner completing a consolidated approval is the same person who initiated the hire, that approval step is automatically skipped.
🚫
Excluded from this DashboardFederal Work Study positions and State-specific payroll steps have been excluded. All flows shown apply to City positions only.

Role Definitions
Security role descriptions for Hire/Rehire — Staff & Faculty scope
Role Description Can Wear Other Hats?
HR Partner Primary actor across nearly every step of the process — background checks, org assignments, compensation, personal info, IDs, and post-completion steps. Typical initiator of Direct Hires. ✔ Can also be Compensation Partner or Budget Manager
HR Administrator Broader administrative role that can also initiate or act on hire steps, depending on security configuration. ✔ Can also be HR Partner or HR Specialist
HR Specialist Can initiate or act on hire steps in supporting capacity, depending on security configuration. ✔ Can also be HR Partner or HR Administrator
Recruiter Marks a candidate "Ready for Hire" in Recruiting, which routes the hire directly to the HR Partner's inbox. ✔ Can also be Recruiting Administrator
Recruiting Administrator Administrative oversight of the recruiting-to-hire handoff. ✔ Can also be Recruiter
Manager Can be an initiator on the hire process depending on security setup for the supervisory organization. ✔ Can hold other roles
Payroll Partner Assigns pay group, adds city payroll data, checks budget (full-time), and gives the final consolidated approval that completes the hire. Central-office role — campuses cannot proxy as Payroll Partner. ✔ Can also be Payroll Administrator
Payroll Administrator Supporting administrative role for payroll-related steps. ✔ Can also be Payroll Partner
Budget Manager Gives consolidated approval for part-time positions, reviewing appointment and professional hours. Tied to the company associated with the supervisory organization. ✔ Can hold other roles
Compensation Partner Gives consolidated approval when the hire was initiated by someone else and the job classification requires compensation review. ✔ Can hold other roles
Academic Faculty Partner Adds the academic appointment for part-time adjunct positions after the hire is complete. ✔ Can hold other roles
Workday Service System-driven step — not a human role. Creates/resets the Workday account and kicks off the onboarding business process automatically. N/A — automated
Employee Completes self-service onboarding tasks (contact/personal info, I-9, emergency contacts) once their Workday account is created. N/A
🚫
Excluded: Federal Work Study & State PositionsFWS hiring and State-specific payroll roles/steps are not included in this dashboard. These operate under a separate scope and process.
