Managing Academic Appointments — At a Glance
3 Core Business Processes · Academic Unit Scope · Staff/Faculty

Add Academic Appointment
Academic Faculty Partner adds a new appointment for a worker — standalone or as a subprocess of Hire / Add Additional Job.
✏️
Update Academic Appointment
Academic Faculty Partner updates an existing appointment — standalone or as a subprocess of Change Job.
⏹
End Academic Appointment
Academic Faculty Partner ends an appointment — standalone or as a subprocess of Termination / End Additional Job.
Supervisory Orgs vs. Academic Units
Why only an Academic Faculty Partner can act here
Supervisory Organization
  • Reflects who reports to who (reporting structure)
  • Supports staffing BPs: Hire, Compensation, Termination
  • Superior/subordinate paths depend on managers
  • Reflects position & position incumbent data
Academic Unit
  • Structure for appointments, instructor eligibility, course assignments
  • Superior/subordinate paths derive from how student careers are managed (e.g., College over Department)
  • Defines student functionality features (majors, applications)
  • Appoints workers to be involved with academic activity
ℹ️
Two Separate Org StructuresSupervisory Orgs and Academic Units are entirely separate hierarchies in Workday. A person cannot be assigned "HR Partner" security on an Academic Unit — they must separately hold the Academic Faculty Partner role, even if they already hold HR Partner at the Supervisory Org level. Security role mapping for Academic Faculty Partner is done per campus/academic unit.
Security Roles Legend
Who participates across all Academic Appointment processes
🟣 Academic Faculty Partner 🔵 HR Partner (related process only) 🌊 Payroll Partner (related process only)
⚠️
Single-Role, Single-Step ProcessesAll three Academic Appointment business processes (Add, Update, End) have only one step — initiation and submission by the Academic Faculty Partner. There is no separate reviewer or approver step; the process auto-completes the moment it is submitted.
🔀
Standalone vs. SubprocessEvery Academic Appointment BP can be launched two ways: (1) as a standalone, ad hoc task by the Academic Faculty Partner via the search bar or a worker's Academic action menu, or (2) automatically as a sub-step inside a related Supervisory-Org business process (Hire, Add Additional Job, Change Job, Termination, End Additional Job).
💰
Appointment Hours Live ElsewhereAppointment hours and professional hours are not fields on the Academic Appointment form. They're tracked on a separate Budget Data custom object, entered by the HR Partner as a step within Hire or Change Job. Academic Appointment tracks dates, rank/title, track type, and tenure — not hours, pay, or job details.
🚫
Excluded From This DashboardFederal Work Study appointments and State-specific position steps are out of scope and not represented here. All flows shown apply to City/CUNY academic (Staff/Faculty) positions within TAM scope only.
🗒️

Two-Step Reappointment PatternFor adjunct reappointments driven by a Change Job, there are effectively two separate steps: HR Partner updates hours on Budget Data, then the Change Job flow prompts the Academic Faculty Partner to update the Academic Appointment (dates, rank).

Role Definitions
Security role descriptions for Managing Academic Appointments — TAM scope
Role Description Can Wear Other Hats?
Academic Faculty Partner The only role that can initiate, edit, or submit Add / Update / End Academic Appointment. Assigned per Academic Unit (not per Supervisory Org), so it must be granted separately even to people who already hold HR Partner. Receives these steps in-inbox when they occur as a subprocess of Hire, Add Additional Job, Change Job, Termination, or End Additional Job — and can also perform them ad hoc. ✔ Can also be HR Partner, but the Academic Faculty Partner role must be separately assigned
HR Partner Not part of the Academic Appointment business processes themselves. Enters appointment & professional hours on the Budget Data custom object as a step within Hire or Change Job (since hours have no field on the appointment form itself). ✔ Can also be Academic Faculty Partner if separately assigned
Payroll Partner Not part of the Academic Appointment business processes. Updates payroll data as a related step within Hire / Change Job. ✔ Can also hold other roles
⚠️
No Approval Step ExistsUnlike many Workday business processes, none of the three Academic Appointment processes route to a reviewer or approver. The Academic Faculty Partner's submission is the final action — the process auto-completes.
🔀
What Academic Appointments Are NotAcademic Appointments are not Positions, not Course/Course Sections, and do not automatically update compensation, pay, or job details. They exist to track an appointee's time at the institution, tenure, academic relationship to departments/programs, employment classification, and instructor eligibility.
🚫
Excluded: Federal Work Study & State PositionsFWS appointments and State-specific position steps are not included in this dashboard. All flows shown apply to City/CUNY academic (Staff/Faculty) positions within TAM scope only.
