Security Roles Legend
Who participates across the Onboarding process
🔴 Employee (incl. Pre-Employee / Contingent Worker as Self) 🔵 HR Partner ⚪ Business Process Administrator
ℹ️
One Business Process, Many StepsOnboarding is a single Workday business process made up of a sequence of configurable steps (tasks). The process is considered technically complete once the worker adds Government IDs and Personal Information — remaining to-do steps (benefits, training, etc.) stay in the worker's inbox independently until each is finished, but they do not block the hire or the BP completion status.
⚠️
Pre-Hire Access Depends on Start DateIf a new hire's start date is in the future, they get limited pre-hire access — only permitted onboarding tasks and permitted announcements/bulletins are visible. If the start date is today, the worker gets full access to complete all onboarding tasks and see most announcements/bulletins.
🔀
Several Steps Are ConditionalSprintax (international status), Passport/Visa, Emergency Evacuation Assistance, Education, and Pay Hiring Fee for Classified Titles only appear for workers who meet the specific condition (see Conditional Steps tab).
🚫
Excluded from this DashboardFederal Work Study positions and State-specific steps have been excluded — this dashboard reflects Staff/Faculty onboarding only. Third-party integration icons/branding are not shown; this is a Workday-configuration view.

Form I-9 & Employment Eligibility

Section 1: Employee as Self · Section 2: HR Partner

Must Complete Within3 Business Days
Who is Involved
  • Employee Completes Section 1 — personal info, citizenship status, preparer/translator
  • HR Partner Completes Section 2 in person — reviews List A or B&C documents, submits to E-Verify, authorizes or sends back
Decision Flags
⚠️
3-Day Legal DeadlineThe employee must present I-9 documents within 3 days of hire. If they never do, the job may ultimately need to be terminated (I-9 is only applicable to a worker's first/primary job — additional jobs for existing workers don't need a new I-9).
🔀
In-Person RequirementSection 2 must be completed in person — the HR Partner physically reviews the original documents against what the employee entered.

Role Definitions
Security role descriptions for Onboarding — Staff/Faculty scope
Role Description Notes
Employee (as Self) Covers Employee, Pre-Employee, Contingent Worker, and Pre-Contingent Worker as Self. The initiator of nearly every onboarding step — completes their own tasks from their Workday inbox / My Tasks. Account credentials (SSO) are created upon hire so the worker can log in and complete onboarding.
HR Partner Reviews and completes Section 2 of Form I-9 in person, may pre-enter government ID/SSN before hire, and reviews/approves the Voluntary Self-ID of Disability submission. Access is scoped to the HR Partner's own supervisory org / campus. Multiple HR Coordinators can be assigned the HR Partner role. Central HR Partners can see across organizations; campus-level HR Partners are limited to their own suborg.
Business Process Administrator Receives and reviews Emergency Evacuation Assistance Requests submitted by employees. Visibility is limited to those responsible for the emergency evacuation plan. Distinct from general BP configuration/admin access — scoped to this specific step.
⚠️
Open Item: Classified-Title Condition RuleThe condition rule driving the Hiring Fee step uses job classifications supplied by the Civil Service team. Some edge cases (e.g. specific adjunct titles) still need confirmation/testing per session Q&A.
⚠️
Open Item: Medical Clearance Form (Classified Hires)A campus raised that some classified hires require a separate medical clearance form. This is not currently a configured Workday step — it would need defined trigger timing and applicable roles/conditions before being added.
🚫
Excluded: Federal Work Study & State PositionsFWS onboarding and State-specific roles are not included in this dashboard. These operate under a separate scope and process.
