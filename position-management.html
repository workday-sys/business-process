Position Management at a Glance
6 Business Processes · City Positions · Excluding Federal Work Study & State-Only Steps
➕
Create Position
Full lifecycle from initiation to Budget Manager approval. Includes org assignments & compensation.
✏️
Edit Position Restrictions
Modify existing position details. Routes based on full-time vs part-time status.
📊
Edit Additional Data
Edit custom fields & payroll data for city positions (non-FWS employees).
❄️
Freeze / Unfreeze
Temporarily suspend a position. Same task used for both freeze and unfreeze.
🔒
Close Position
Permanently close a city position. Dual-approval by HR Partner and Budget Manager.
🔧
Edit Position (Minor Changes)
For small, non-structural edits (e.g., title change). Does NOT go through Budget Manager. Major changes use Change Job instead.

Key Decision Points & Conditions
Flags to know before testing or using these processes
⚠️
Self-Initiated = Review SkippedApplies to: Create Position, Edit Position Restrictions, Edit Position. When the HR Partner initiates, they bypass their own "Review" step because they already know the request.
🔀
Full-Time vs Part-Time — Edit Position RestrictionsFull-time positions route budget data editing to the HR Partner. Part-time positions route it to the Manager. This was changed in A&C2.
🆕
A&C2 Change: Payroll Partner Added to Additional Data BPPayroll Partners now have a required step in the Edit Position Restrictions Additional Data process for city/Employee/non-FWS positions.
🆕
A&C2 Change: Budget Manager Owns Budget Data (Additional Data BP)Previously the Manager edited budget data for part-time in this process. Now Budget Manager handles it for all non-FWS Employee positions.
🚫
Edit Position ≠ Change JobEdit Position is only for minor changes (title). Any changes to job profile, job classification, or similar structural data must go through the separate Change Job process.
🚫
Edit Position does NOT involve Budget ManagerDespite what you might expect, Edit Position only requires HR Partner approval — not Budget Manager sign-off.
🗑️
Excluded: Federal Work Study & State-Specific StepsFWS Coordinator roles and State payroll data steps have been removed from this dashboard per your request. Contact your FWS team for those workflows.
