Change Benefits

Who is Involved

Change Benefits for Life

Completes When

Who is Involved

  • Benefits Partner Can initiate; final approval & document review

Key Notes
💡
Domestic Partner Affidavit RequiredIf employee is adding a domestic partner, a Domestic Partner Affidavit must be completed as part of this process.
🐍
May Trigger COBRAIf the life event reduces coverage eligibility (e.g., divorce removes a dependent), a COBRA eligibility event is automatically generated.
⚠️
Benefits Partner Cannot Update Worker DemographicsChanges to employee name, gender, or date of birth must be done by the HR Partner — not the Benefits Partner.

Review COBRA Eligibility

Triggered by termination or loss of coverage in benefits eligibility — processes COBRA notice

Completes When
Benefits Partner Completes To-Do
Who is Involved
  • Benefits Administrator Can initiate; completes COBRA to-do step
  • Benefits Partner Can initiate; reviews eligibility; sends COBRA packet
  • Employee (Self) Can initiate; reviews & acknowledges COBRA document
  • HR Partner Can initiate; handles separation/termination side
Key Notes
⚡
When It TriggersCOBRA eligibility is processed when there is either a termination event or a loss of coverage in benefits eligibility (e.g., divorce, dependent aging out).
📋
COBRA Packet = Review Document StepThe employee receives a "Review Documents" task — not just a notification. They must download, acknowledge, and submit to confirm they received the COBRA notice.
💡
Auto-PopulationThe COBRA eligibility reason, coverage dates, and duration are automatically populated by the system based on the benefit plan and event type.
Process Flowchart
System / Benefits Administrator / Benefits Partner / HR Partner
🚀 INITIATION — Termination or Loss of Coverage Occurs
↓
System (Auto)
COBRA Eligibility Auto-Populated (Reason, Eligibility Date, Coverage Duration)
↓
Benefits Partner / Benefits Administrator
Review COBRA Eligibility Details — Approve
↓
Employee (or Former Employee)
Review Documents Step — Download COBRA Notice, Acknowledge & Submit
↓
Benefits Partner / Benefits Administrator
Complete To-Do: Send COBRA Packet to Individual (via Email or Mail)
↓
✅ COMPLETE — COBRA Process Finalized
📌
Step Types in This BPThis process contains all three step types: an Approval step (Benefits Partner reviews eligibility), a Review Document step (Employee acknowledges COBRA notice), and a To-Do step (Benefits Partner sends the packet).

Change Benefits for Open Enrollment

Annual open enrollment initiated and finalized by the central Benefits Administrator team

Completes When
Benefits Admin Closes & Finalizes
Who is Involved
  • Benefits Administrator ONLY initiator, closer & finalizer of Open Enrollment
  • Benefits Partner Assists employees; reviews docs; approves events at campus
  • HR Partner Not directly involved in open enrollment steps
Key Notes
🏛️
Central Team Controls InitiationOnly the Benefits Administrator (central team) can launch, close, and finalize the Open Enrollment event. Benefits Partners at campuses cannot initiate enrollment periods.
🔀
Passive vs. Active EnrollmentPassive: Carries over current elections — employees only need to act for FSA/dependent care. Active: Wipes all elections — every employee must re-enroll.
📄
New Dependent During Open EnrollmentIf an employee adds a new dependent during enrollment, they must upload dependent verification documentation. Benefits Partner reviews eligibility before approving.

Key Decision Points & Conditions
Flags to know before configuring or using these processes
⏱️
30-Day Rule for Life EventsEmployees have 30 days from a qualifying life event to complete elections and submit any required documentation. Benefits Partners can correct events after approval if needed.
🔀
Change Benefits → Change Benefits for LifeThe Change Benefits BP serves as the entry point or "request" that leads into the Change Benefits for Life sub-process when a specific qualifying life event triggers benefit eligibility changes.
🐍
COBRA is Triggered AutomaticallyWhen a Dependent Event or Change Benefits for Life reduces coverage eligibility, the system auto-populates COBRA eligibility reason, start date, and coverage duration. Benefits Partners do NOT manually create COBRA records.
📋
Three Step Types in Benefits BPsApproval: Benefits Partner reviews and approves. Review Document: Employee downloads and acknowledges COBRA notice. To-Do: Benefits Partner reminder to send COBRA packet (email or mail).
⏸
Passive vs Active Open EnrollmentPassive enrollment carries forward current elections (employees only act for FSA). Active enrollment wipes all elections and requires every employee to re-enroll. Central team chooses the type when initiating.
🔗
Integration File Timing — Vendor UpdatesOnce enrollment is approved, data is sent to vendors via integration files on a vendor-specific schedule (weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly). In emergencies, Benefits Partners can manually update vendor portals while awaiting the next file run.
⚠️
Benefits Partner Cannot Change Worker DemographicsName, date of birth, and gender on the worker record are owned by HR, not Benefits. Changes must be made by the HR Partner via HCM processes. Benefits Partner can only correct benefit enrollment data.
🚫
Excluded: Federal Work Study & State-Specific StepsFWS Coordinator roles and all State employee-specific benefit steps have been removed from this dashboard per configuration requirements. Contact the appropriate team for those workflows.
